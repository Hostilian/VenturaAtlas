# EUshop DAC7 Tax Reporting Engine Skill

## Overview

This skill guides seller classification, gross consideration calculation, annual reporting aggregation, and XML reporting under EU DAC7 Directive.

---

## 1. Reporting Thresholds & Data Boundaries

- **Thresholds**: 30 completed transactions OR €2,000 gross consideration in a calendar year (`DAC7_THRESHOLDS`).
- **Required Data**: Seller Name, Tax Identification Number (TIN), Primary Address, Bank Account Identifier (IBAN), Quarterly Gross Consideration, Platform Fees.
