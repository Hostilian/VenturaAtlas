QUICK START — FIVE THINGS TO DO RIGHT NOW
------------------------------------------
1. Sort by "Overall score" to see the highest-rated ideas
2. Use chip filter "$0 Start" to find zero-capital ideas
3. Use the Venture Matcher wizard (homepage or docs/matcher.html) to filter by your skills & budget
4. Click "Random" to discover a surprise idea
5. Download the sanitized public ideas.json projection to work offline in your AI assistant


SEARCH BAR TIPS (press "/" to focus)
--------------------------------------
The search bar searches: idea name, problem statement, customer description, tags, and category.

Useful search queries:
  "compliance eu"         → EU regulatory compliance tools
  "ai agent"             → Autonomous AI agent products
  "marketplace trust"    → Trust/verification marketplace ideas
  "food knowledge graph" → Food data & knowledge systems
  "zero cost bootstrap"  → Zero-capital startup ideas
  "game language"        → Language learning through gaming
  "repair sustainability"→ Product repair / circular economy
  "research automation"  → AI-automated research tools

Tips:
  - Use multiple words (results are ANDed)
  - Search is case-insensitive
  - Combine with a category filter for narrower results
  - Press Esc to clear the search bar


FILTER STRATEGIES
-----------------
BY CATEGORY:
  AI / Autonomous Agents         → Pure AI/ML product businesses
  EU Compliance & Regulation     → EU-law based services
  Food & Beverage                → Food tech, knowledge graphs, evidence registries
  Developer Tools                → APIs, SDKs, CLI tools
  Marketplace                    → Two-sided markets, trust layers, seller tools
  Education                      → Language learning, Erasmus tools, course matching

BY STATUS:
  Priority    → Top-ranked ideas; most complete data
  Researched  → Fully documented with financial models and validation plans
  Explore     → Early-stage ideas worth investigating
  Archived    → Deprioritized but preserved for reference

COMBINING FILTERS:
  Example: Category=AI Tools + Status=Priority + Sort=Lowest Cost
  = the cheapest high-priority AI ideas

VENTURE MATCHER WIZARD
-----------------------
Quick version on homepage. Full version at docs/matcher.html.

Three quick questions:
  1. Budget? ($0, $100-$1k, $1k-$5k, $5k+)
  2. How fast do you need revenue? (Days, weeks, months)
  3. Founder strength? (Solo, Technical, Non-Technical, AI Specialist)

Full matcher adds:
  - Risk tolerance
  - Hours per week
  - Technical skill level
  - Geographic focus (EU / Global)
  - Target customer type (B2B / B2C / B2B2C)

COMPARING IDEAS
---------------
1. In Cards or Table view, select 2–4 ideas with their checkboxes
2. Click "Compare selected" in the viewbar
3. Side-by-side comparison opens at docs/compare.html

Or go directly to docs/compare.html and pick ideas from dropdowns.
Comparison shows: scores, customer types, startup costs, revenue speed, evidence labels, tags.

FINDING NEW IDEAS TO ADD
-------------------------
Process:
  1. Start with a problem — what pain exists in a market you understand?
  2. Check if it's already here — search the directory for related keywords
  3. Validate the gap — is it genuinely novel or a variant of an existing idea?
  4. Use MASTER_SEARCH_PROMPT.md in your AI assistant
  5. Score it using docs/methodology.html
  6. Submit it following docs/ADDING_AN_IDEA.md

Sources for new ideas:
  - AI assistants: "What underserved markets appeared in the last 12 months?"
  - Reddit: r/SaaS, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur
  - Product Hunt trends
  - EU regulatory calendar (new regulations = compliance tool opportunities)
  - GitHub Trending (developer pain points become developer tools)
  - Job boards (mass hiring for painful manual tasks = automation opportunity)


VALIDATING AN IDEA — FAST FRAMEWORK (48 hours)
-----------------------------------------------
Day 1 — Evidence gathering:
  1. Open the idea dossier (ideas/[idea-slug].md)
  2. Read the validation plan (validation-plans/[idea-slug].md)
  3. Run the validation prompts from docs/prompts.html
  4. Google the problem — find 5 people complaining about it publicly

Day 2 — Market check:
  1. Find 3 existing competitors or partial solutions
  2. Identify 1 differentiating angle vs. competitors
  3. Calculate a realistic revenue scenario at docs/calculator.html
  4. Write down 3 assumptions that could be wrong

Validation checklist:
  [ ] Problem is real (5+ public complaints found)
  [ ] Customer is specific and reachable
  [ ] Existing solutions are inadequate
  [ ] Revenue model is clear (who pays, how much, when)
  [ ] Startup cost is within budget
  [ ] First customer acquisition path identified
  [ ] You have a founder-edge (skill, network, or insight)
