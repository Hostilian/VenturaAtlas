## 2. Selection Rationale: Why `idea-061`?

1. **Non-Discretionary Budget & Regulatory Urgency**: Multi-tenant compliance reporting is driven by mandatory vendor security questionnaires (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, DORA) where buyers have established budget line items rather than discretionary nice-to-have spending.
2. **High Willingness to Pay & Unit Economics**: Rated **8.5/10 WTP** across all evaluators. Target contract sizes (\$5,000–\$25,000 ARR) allow profitable direct founder-led sales before paid acquisition.
3. **Rapid MVP Feasibility**: Architecture allows a narrow beachhead MVP (automated evidence collection + schema-validated export packet) to be built in under 4 weeks using existing infrastructure.
4. **Founder-Idea Alignment**: Strong technical domain fit for the founding engineering and compliance backgrounds.

---

## 3. Decisive Assumptions (What must be true?)

For this venture decision to remain valid, the following core assumptions must hold true:
- **Assumption 1 (Buyer Need)**: B2B SaaS and fintech engineering teams spend $>15$ hours per month manually compiling audit evidence for prospective enterprise buyers.
- **Assumption 2 (Budget Authority)**: Heads of Security or Compliance possess corporate credit cards or discretionary signing authority for tools $<\$1,000$/month without requiring 9-month procurement committee cycles.
- **Assumption 3 (Differentiation)**: Incumbent horizontal compliance automation platforms (e.g. Vanta, Drata) leave a substantial gap in developer-first multi-tenant API integrations and custom audit trail export formats.

## 4. Recorded Dissent & Minority Objections

To protect against groupthink and preserve critical feedback:
- **Commercial Risk (Jordan)**: Enterprise sales cycles could stretch beyond 6 months if buyers require SOC 2 Type II certification from us prior to deploying our audit agent.
- **Platform Threat (Taylor)**: Horizontal CRM and cloud providers might bundle basic automated audit logs into their native dashboards over the next 24 months.
- **Technical Dealbreaker Flag (Alex)**: Audit logs and evidence records must guarantee tamper-evident cryptographic hashing from day one to withstand SOC 2 auditor scrutiny.
