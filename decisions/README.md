## Decision Governance Framework

Per [`collaboration/GOVERNANCE.md`](../collaboration/GOVERNANCE.md) and [`collaboration/DECISION_PROCESS.md`](../collaboration/DECISION_PROCESS.md):
1. **Evidence vs Opinion**: Personal votes and scorecards are recorded transparently and never mutate canonical research benchmarks.
2. **Immutability & Lineage**: Decisions receive stable identifiers (`DEC-XXXX`). A decision is never deleted; if falsified by real-world validation tests, it is reopened through a linked successor record.
3. **Dissent Preservation**: All minority objections, dealbreakers, and decisive assumptions must be explicitly documented in the decision record.
