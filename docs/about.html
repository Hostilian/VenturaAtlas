About Venture Atlas OS

Venture Atlas OS is an open research repository and intelligence platform preserving 70 canonical business ideas, 62 cited sources, and 1,750 AI prompt templates across 8 deep research sessions.

Core Principles

    Never lose an idea, never repeat an idea: Every idea discovered across research rounds is preserved as a distinct, scored canonical record. Evidence over opinion: Claims are backed by primary source citations, government studies, and protocol standards. Zero-Dollar Founder Realism: Every idea evaluates real-world execution feasibility under strict capital ($0–$100) and time-to-revenue constraints. Complete transparency: Scores are decision-support tools, financial numbers are transparent scenarios, and unverified assumptions are explicitly labeled.
