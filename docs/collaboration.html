Collaboration & Governance

Collaborative Decision Studio

Venture Atlas OS provides an offline-first Decision Studio to discover, shortlist, evaluate, fork, and decide on startup opportunities with co-founders. A browser-local decision room does not synchronize state across devices unless connected to a configured Firebase room.

🚀 Launch Decision Studio

Create a collaborative workspace, score candidates on 10 dimensions, run head-to-head tournaments, design idea variants, and declare provisional winners.

Open Decision Studio →
📋

Pipeline Kanban & Stages

Organize candidates across 7 structured stages: Inbox, Interesting, Researching, Finalist, Testing, Winner, and Parked.

  • 1-click shortlist addition from search or dossiers
  • Structured stages prevent prematurely locking decisions
  • Tagged ideas and custom sorting
📊

10-D Scorecards & Disagreement

Multi-criteria scoring covering Pain Severity, WTP, Distribution, Founder Fit, Speed to MVP, Capital Efficiency, and AI leverage.

  • Automated Team Consensus & Disagreement alerts
  • Identifies high-variance dimensions among co-founders
  • Subjective scores never pollute canonical research
⚔️

Pairwise Tournaments

Head-to-head comparison matchups forcing explicit tradeoffs ("If you could only build one, which?").

  • Automated combination generation
  • Leaderboard with win rates and match statistics
  • Rationale capture for qualitative context
💡

Idea Variants Lab

Fork canonical ideas into tailored versions with specific buyer wedges, pricing tweaks, or narrower MVP scopes.

  • Branch lineage tree: Idea → Variant A → Variant B
  • Compare customized variants directly against parent
  • Preserves canonical evidence provenance
🏆

Provisional Winner & Rationale

Record formal team decisions with decisive assumptions, dissent capture, and review triggers.

  • Documents why an idea won over other finalists
  • Explicitly preserves minority objections
  • Defined reconsideration triggers for reopening
🧪

Validation Experiments Loop

Design fast, cheap falsification tests before investing months into product engineering.

  • Track hypothesis, test design, target metrics, and budget
  • Log real-world outcomes and pivot decisions
  • Closes the venture-learning loop

Storage & Synchronization Architecture

Local-First Storage

All workspaces, shortlists, notes, scorecards, variants, and decisions are stored locally in your browser. Fully functional offline with zero cloud dependency.

Cloud Firestore Sync (Optional)

When configured in assets/js/config.js, VenturaAtlas connects to Firebase Anonymous Auth and Cloud Firestore for real-time multiplayer room synchronization.

Decision Packets (.json)

Export a complete, self-contained decision packet as JSON. Share asynchronously with co-founders or import into another browser context anytime.
