What the labels mean

Published idea
A structured hypothesis in the main directory. It can still contain gaps.
Fresh idea
An early proposal in the research catalog. It has not earned a score or recommendation.
Legacy score
An older heuristic retained for provenance. Do not compare it blindly across records.
Evidence confidence
How well the important claims are supported—not the chance that the business will succeed.
