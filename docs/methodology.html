Scoring Methodology

Every idea in Venture Atlas OS is evaluated across 25 standardized criteria, scored from 0.0 to 10.0 with explicit justification, confidence rating, and evidence basis.

The 25 Scoring Criteria

Problem Severity
Quantifies the painfulness, time loss, or financial cost of the unaddressed problem.
Frequency of Need
How often the target customer experiences the pain point (daily, weekly, per purchase).
Willingness to Pay
Evidence of budget allocation or payment for adjacent solutions.
Market Demand
Current search volume, forum activity, and structural buying signals.
Market Growth
Industry growth trends, regulatory tailwinds, and macro shifts (e.g. agentic AI).
Revenue Potential
Realistic ARR ceiling based on unit economics and addressable customer count.
Recurring Potential
Subscription, usage, or repeat transaction characteristics of the model.
Gross Margin
Estimated gross margin percentage after variable infrastructure and support costs.
Speed to First Revenue
Estimated time from day 1 to collecting the first paid dollar.
Low Startup Cost
Score inverse to required capital budget (10 = $0-$100 startup cost).
Ease of MVP
Simplicity of building the minimum viable testable deliverable.
AI Automation Potential
Degree to which LLMs, Vision AI, and agents can automate core workflows.
Ease of Distribution
Reachability of target buyers via organic, community, or direct channels.
Retention Potential
Stickiness of the product and natural switching costs.
Competitive Advantage
Inverse of competitive density (high score = low competitive intensity).
Defensibility
Moats created by network effects, proprietary data, or workflow integration.
Data Advantage
Compounding value of proprietary datasets or evidence graphs accumulated over time.
Scalability
Ability to handle 10x volume without proportional headcount increase.
Founder Accessibility
Ease with which a solo founder or small team can operate the venture.
Regulatory Simplicity
Freedom from heavy licensing, legal barriers, or complex compliance burdens.
Operational Simplicity
Low ongoing customer support, manual review, or logistics requirements.
Global Potential
Ability to serve international customers without localized physical infrastructure.
Timing Signal
Macro technological or protocol inflection point alignment (e.g., 2026 agentic commerce).
Evidence Quality
Reliability and primary-source backing of research claims.
Overall Confidence
Analyst confidence level combining all verified evidence and unknown risks.
