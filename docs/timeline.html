Methodology
Zero-Dollar Founder Realism
Every idea is evaluated for feasibility under $0–$100 startup capital and solo founder execution. Startup costs above $5,000 receive a realism penalty in the easeOfMvp score.
Evidence Labeling
Claims in each idea dossier carry one of four labels: CONFIRMED (primary source cited), PLAUSIBLE (analogous evidence), UNVERIFIED (author reasoning only), CONTRADICTED (opposing evidence found). Only CONFIRMED claims count toward the confidence score.
Score dimensions
Each idea is scored across 25 dimensions (1–10 scale) grouped into: Revenue Ceiling, Cost & Speed, Differentiation, Evidence Quality, and Founder Fit. The overall score is a weighted harmonic mean, not an average.
Ranking runs
31 independent ranking runs cover different dimensions (e.g., "Top 10 by profit for solo founder", "Top 10 for non-technical founder", "Top 10 by fastest revenue"). Rankings are stored immutably — older runs are never deleted when new ones are added.
Prompt packs
Each idea has exactly 25 Markdown prompts covering: market validation, MVP specification, customer interview scripts, competitive analysis, pricing experiments, launch copy, and failure mode analysis.
