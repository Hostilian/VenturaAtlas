# 3PL Activity-to-Invoice Grid Dossier

## Overview
Logistics WMS activity event to invoice audit engine verifying 3PL pick, pack, storage, and handling fee billing accuracy.

## Target Customer
E-commerce brands and logistics managers outsourcing fulfillment to 3PLs.

## Problem Solved
Hidden billing leakage in 3PL invoices for storage surcharges, special handling, and packing materials.
