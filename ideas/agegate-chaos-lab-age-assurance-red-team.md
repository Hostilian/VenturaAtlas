# AgeGate Chaos Lab — Adversarial Age Assurance Fail-Safe & Integration Red Team (idea-309)

**Score:** 85/100  |  **Category:** Online Safety & Identity

## Executive Summary
Controlled adversarial testing harness that exercises age-verification integrations against vendor timeouts, state mutations, and session leaks.

## Detailed Concept
UK Ofcom enforcement (fining platforms £1.35M+ for age-check failures) explicitly states that using third-party vendors does not remove platform liability. AgeGate Chaos Lab injects synthetic edge-case journeys (device switching, vendor timeouts, fallback routes, multi-account linking) to verify fail-closed behavior and privacy compliance.
