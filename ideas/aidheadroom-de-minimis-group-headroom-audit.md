# AidHeadroom -- De Minimis Group Headroom Audit

> **OMEGA Round**: OMEGA XVII | **Research date**: 2026-08-17 | **Score**: 87.0/100 | **Status**: priority

## One-sentence concept

For EU grant advisers and multi-company applicants, AidHeadroom resolves the corporate group counting as one undertaking, assembles de minimis awards from public registers plus legacy declarations, calculates remaining three-year headroom by Member State, and produces a source-linked review pack before the next aid application.

## Target customer

Independent EU grant consultants managing applications for corporate clients

## Problem solved

The de minimis ceiling applies to the entire corporate group as one undertaking. Assembling the group perimeter, historical awards, and new 2026 register entries into a defensible evidence trail is genuinely painful.
