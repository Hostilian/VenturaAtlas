# Battery Afterlife Exchange (idea-270)

**Score:** 8.0/10  |  **Category:** Battery & Energy Storage Infrastructure

## Executive Summary
Second-life battery valuation and auction platform leveraging mandatory Digital Battery Passport data to match used EV packs with stationary storage integrators.

## Product Description
Data-driven battery resale and recycling marketplace converting state-of-health, charge history, and safety data into second-life storage valuations.
