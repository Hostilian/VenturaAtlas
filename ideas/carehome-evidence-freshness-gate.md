## Executive Summary

Surfaces the exact age of care home inspection ratings (e.g. UK CQC inspections running on average 7 years out of date) and layers recent public indicators (complaint filings, staff turnover job postings) so families selecting care for aging relatives do not rely on stale ratings.

## Regulatory & Market Catalyst

- **Inspection Lag:** UK CQC publishes ~2,076 care ratings/year against 15,000 locations (~7-year gap). CQC chief executive publicly admitted ratings run years out of date. Published ratings dropped from 13,505 in 2019/20 to 4,447 in 2023/24.
- **High Stakes:** Families making high-cost decisions (£1,000+/week) lack visibility into whether a "Good" rating from 2018 reflects current conditions.

## Product Concept

1. **Rating Age Prominence:** Displays CQC/Welsh/Scottish rating along with explicit staleness metrics ("Inspected 2,410 days ago").
2. **Recent Signal Overlay:** Ingests recent secondary signals (local authority complaint logs, job vacancy surges, CQC enforcement notices).
3. **Freshness Assessment:** Generates an overall rating confidence score.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Families seeking eldercare, private care placement advisors.
- **Pricing:** Free public lookup; £29 for detailed home evidence reports.
