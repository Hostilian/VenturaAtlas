## 1. Concept
Job-level cash feasibility engine for trade contractors and commercial service providers. It estimates: *"If I accept this project, under which payment scenarios does cash fall below payroll, and with what probability?"*

An exact zero-cash date would be false precision because payment timing is uncertain. Float, Dryrun, Cash Flow Frog, and Siteline already cover substantial parts of forecasting, scenarios, construction billing, and collections. Keep only if one contractor niche demonstrates that job-acceptance modelling is not solved by those substitutes.
