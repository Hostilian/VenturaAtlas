# CatchLint / BorderPreflight — Digital Customs & CATCH Preflight Compiler Dossier

## Overview
Static linter and validation compiler checking digital seafood CATCH certificates, vessel IDs, and lot weights against EU border parser rules before shipment dispatch.

## Target Customer
Seafood exporters, international freight forwarders, customs brokers, and food importers.

## Problem Solved
Containers stranded at EU ports due to schema mismatches, vessel ID typos, and mass imbalance errors in mandatory CATCH digital filings.
