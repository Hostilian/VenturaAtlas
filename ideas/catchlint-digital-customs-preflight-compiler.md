## Original Stub (preserved for provenance)

### Overview
Static linter and validation compiler checking digital seafood CATCH certificates, vessel IDs, and lot weights against EU border parser rules before shipment dispatch.

### Target Customer
Seafood exporters, international freight forwarders, customs brokers, and food importers.

### Problem Solved
Containers stranded at EU ports due to schema mismatches, vessel ID typos, and mass imbalance errors in mandatory CATCH digital filings.

---

## Why the Thesis Was Upgraded

The OMEGA XVII-B research pass (18 August 2026) found:

1. CATCH became mandatory on 10 January 2026
2. On 4 August 2026, the EU Market Advisory Council called for operational stabilisation — citing recurring technical failures, clearance delays, and missing interoperability
3. The durable problem is not schema validation alone; it is **cross-system certificate lineage and quantity reconciliation** across vessel → catch → certificate → processor → importer → CATCH → customs
4. The correct MVP is a software-assisted service (manual preflight of real shipments), not a static linter

See [idea-415](./catchflow-catch-preflight-certificate-lineage.md) for the full dossier.
