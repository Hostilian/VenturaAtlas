# CMMC Enclave Escape Test — Controlled CUI Boundary & Drift Testing Engine (idea-316)

**Score:** 69/100  |  **Category:** Defense & Cybersecurity

## Executive Summary
Automated boundary testing tool that injects controlled synthetic data to detect unauthorized CUI data flow and scope drift outside secure enclave perimeters.

## Detailed Concept
Defense contractors building CMMC enclaves frequently suffer scope expansion when Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) leaks into unmonitored subnets or cloud storage. CMMC Enclave Escape Test deploys benign canary payloads to continuously verify enclave boundaries and prevent unexpected compliance scope creep.
