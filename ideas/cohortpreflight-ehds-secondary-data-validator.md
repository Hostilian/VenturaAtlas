# CohortPreflight Europe — EHDS Secondary Data Cohort & Feasibility Preflight Dossier

## Overview
Secondary health data feasibility preflight engine evaluating cross-country European Health Data Space (EHDS) datasets for study viability before submitting access requests.

## Target Customer
Pharma Real-World Evidence (RWE) teams, contract research organizations (CROs), and academic epidemiologists.

## Problem Solved
Failed multi-country observational studies caused by unannounced gaps in diagnosis coding, missing outcome fields, or low patient overlap across national health data nodes.
