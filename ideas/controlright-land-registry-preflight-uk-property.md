# ControlRight — Contract-to-Land-Registry Preflight & Event Clock (idea-302)

**Score:** 79/100  |  **Category:** Real Estate & Legal Tech

## Executive Summary
Automated data extraction and 60-day event tracking engine for UK contractual land-control arrangements under HM Land Registry digital rules.

## Detailed Concept
Mandatory from April 2027 (with 60-day filing rules applying to rights created from June 2026), UK property law requires conveyancers to submit structured data on options, pre-emptions, and promotion agreements to HM Land Registry. ControlRight extracts mandatory contractual fields, calculates title boundaries, and tracks exercise windows to prevent submission rejections.
