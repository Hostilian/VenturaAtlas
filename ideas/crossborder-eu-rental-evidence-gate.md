## Executive Summary

A verification layer for cross-border renters (Erasmus students, expats, relocating workers) that validates property listings against official registry records and country-specific proof requirements before deposits change hands.

## Market Catalyst

- **High Fraud Volume:** Over €1.75B lost annually to rental scams in the Netherlands alone (2024 data, 1% recovery rate). Similar patterns in Ireland, Germany, Italy, Czech Republic.
- **National Registry Mandates:** Italy introduced mandatory national ID code (CIN) for short-term/rental listings; Germany and Netherlands enforce strict registration and land registry validation.
- **Single-Country Gap:** Existing tools (Stekkies NL, Cohabi IE, Coinquilino IT) are single-country alert tools, failing cross-border movers.

## Product Concept

1. **Listing Authenticity Check:** Reverse image search, land registry cross-referencing, CIN/registry validation.
2. **Landlord Identity Verification:** Document & proof-of-ownership verification.
3. **Escrow / Deposit Safety Checklist:** Country-specific rental contract & payment verification.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Erasmus students, international university departments, expat relocation agencies.
- **Pricing:** €19 per single property verification check or €49 for unlimited monthly search verification.
