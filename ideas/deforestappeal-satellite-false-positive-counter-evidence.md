# DeforestAppeal — Satellite Deforestation False Positive Counter-Evidence Dossier

## Overview
Multi-model satellite land-cover verification engine generating counter-evidence dossiers for false-positive EUDR deforestation alerts.

## Target Customer
Coffee and cocoa importers, agricultural cooperatives, and commodity trading desks.

## Problem Solved
Coarse satellite screening models falsely flagging established tree-crop and agroforestry plots as deforested under EUDR rules.
