# DismantleException — Vehicle Circularity Disproportionate Cost Exception Ledger Dossier

## Overview
Evidence logging tool for auto dismantlers documenting disproportionate labor costs and physical corrosion when required parts removal cannot be completed.

## Target Customer
Automotive dismantlers, ELV recycling facilities, and vehicle shredders.

## Problem Solved
Fines and license revocations for failing to remove mandatory reusable components under strict EU End-of-Life Vehicle circularity rules.
