## Executive Summary

A manual-grade accessibility audit service for small EU e-commerce & digital businesses producing a legally defensible WCAG 2.1 AA evidence pack — bypassing overlay widgets that provide false security and fail legal scrutiny.

## Regulatory Catalyst

- **Enforcement Active:** The European Accessibility Act (EAA) applied across all 27 EU member states on June 28, 2025; 2026 marks the first full year of active national supervision (e.g. French court ordering Carrefour compliance under €500/day penalty).
- **Overlay Discreditation:** Overlay widgets do not satisfy EAA or ADA standards; over 1,400 companies with widgets installed were sued in 2025.
- **Scanner Limitations:** Automated tools detect at best 25–30% of WCAG issues.

## Product Concept

1. **Automated Baseline Scan:** Quick first-pass check for low-hanging WCAG violations.
2. **Targeted Human Verification:** Guided workflow for testing the ~70% of WCAG criteria automated scanners miss (keyboard navigation, screen reader flow, focus order).
3. **Scored Evidence Pack:** Defensible audit report detailing issue locations, remediation steps, and timestamped conformance claims.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** E-commerce store owners, digital agencies, and SME website managers in the EU.
- **Pricing:** €299 per audit or €49/month for quarterly re-audit evidence updates.
