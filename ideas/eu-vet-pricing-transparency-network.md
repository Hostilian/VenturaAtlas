## Executive Summary

A veterinary bill fairness checker and price comparison network for pet owners in continental Europe — bringing mandatory price transparency to EU markets outside the UK.

## Market Context

- **Regulatory Spillover:** UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) investigation forced mandatory price transparency onto UK vets, triggering the launch of VetsCompared (May 2026).
- **Continental Gap:** Vet pricing across France, Germany, Spain, and Central Europe remains highly opaque, with corporate consolidation driving fee inflation without clear price disclosure.

## Product Concept

1. **Bill Upload & Anonymization:** Pet owners upload vet receipts for procedures (surgeries, vaccinations, dental).
2. **Regional Benchmark Comparison:** Compares line items against regional averages and statutory fee scales (e.g. GOT in Germany).
3. **Price Transparency Index:** Ranks veterinary clinics on price transparency and fairness.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Pet owners, pet insurance platforms.
- **Pricing:** Free consumer lookup; B2B API for pet insurers evaluating claim fairness.
