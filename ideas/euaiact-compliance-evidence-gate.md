## Executive Summary

A living, timestamped evidence trail proving which AI systems a small business uses, how each is classified under the EU AI Act (Annex III / general purpose), and whether required documentation and AI-literacy training records are current — replacing unverified spreadsheets before regulators or enterprise clients ask.

## Regulatory Catalyst

- **Enforcement Start:** National-authority enforcement began **August 2, 2026**.
- **No Company Size Floor:** The AI-literacy obligation (Art. 4) applies to a 3-person agency using ChatGPT just as it does to a 300-person enterprise.
- **Fines:** Up to €15M or 3% of global turnover (with SME reduced cap provisions).
- **Moving Law:** Council-adopted Digital Omnibus text (late June 2026) shifts several high-risk deadlines once published, requiring businesses to track current obligations alongside pending legislative changes.

## Problem Statement

Small businesses face an immediate recordkeeping crisis: model inventories live in forgotten spreadsheets, approvals occur over informal chat, risk classifications go stale, and staff use of generative AI tools goes untracked. Enterprise buyers increasingly require AI compliance attestations before signing vendor contracts.

## Product Concept

1. **Intake & Discovery:** Structured questionnaire identifying AI tool usage (ChatGPT, Copilot, Midjourney, internal models).
2. **LLM-Assisted Classification:** Maps each system to EU AI Act risk tiers (Prohibited, High-Risk Annex III, Transparency/GPAI, Minimal Risk).
3. **Evidence Pack Generator:** Outputs timestamped, versioned PDF & portal evidence packs demonstrating compliance effort, AI literacy policies, and model governance.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Founders, Operations Leads, and Compliance Officers at 1–50 person European businesses.
- **Pricing:** €49/month or €399/year subscription for continuous inventory monitoring and legislative timeline updates.
