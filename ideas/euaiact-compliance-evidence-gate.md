## Executive Summary

A living, timestamped evidence trail & localized compliance simplifier proving which AI systems a Czech/CEE small business uses, how each is classified under the EU AI Act, and whether required documentation and AI-literacy training records are current.

**Strategic Pivot:** The generic English-language market for cheap AI Act scanners is already served by existing tools (ComplianceAgent, Legalithm, EuroComply). The defensible edge is a **Czech-language AI Act & Article 50 compliance simplifier**, explicitly mapping which of the **5 Czech enforcement bodies** applies to a given business and translating "AI literacy" (Art. 4) and "synthetic content marking" (Art. 50) into concrete, dated tasks.

## Czech Regulatory Landscape: 5 Enforcement Bodies

Under the Czech Republic's draft Adaptation Act, enforcement is split across five distinct bodies:

1. **ÚNMZ (Úřad pro technickou normalizaci, metrologii a státní zkušebnictví):** Conformity-assessment notifying authority.
2. **ČTÚ (Český telekomunikační úřad):** Primary market surveillance authority and public contact point for general AI Act inquiries.
3. **ÚOOÚ (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů):** Surveillance authority for privacy-related AI aspects and Article 50 biometric/GDPR overlap.
4. **ČNB (Česká národní banka):** Market surveillance for financial-sector AI systems (credit scoring, risk evaluation).
5. **ČAS (Česká agentura pro standardizaci):** National regulatory sandbox coordinator.

## Product Concept & $0–$100 Build Plan

1. **Czech Obligation Intake:** Short questionnaire on company size, AI tools used (ChatGPT, Copilot, Midjourney, custom models), and business use case.
2. **Local Authority Classifier:** Maps responses to the exact Czech enforcement authority (ÚNMZ, ČTÚ, ÚOOÚ, ČNB, ČAS) and applicable legal tier.
3. **Evidence Pack Generator:** Outputs timestamped, versioned Czech-language PDF compliance report containing:
   - Specific Czech authority mapping & contact points.
   - Article 50 chatbot/synthetic content disclosure checklist.
   - Article 4 AI literacy policy template for Czech employees.
   - Timeline calendar tracking upcoming 2027/2028 deadlines.
4. **Distribution & Monetization:**
   - **One-time Fee:** €49 per generated localized compliance pack.
   - **Recurring Plan:** €19/month for continuous monitoring and Czech regulatory transposition alerts.
   - **GTM:** Czech startup hubs, Prague tech networks, Czech SME founder channels.
