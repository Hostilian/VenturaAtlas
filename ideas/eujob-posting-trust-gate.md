## Executive Summary

A dual verification engine for job postings in the EU combining ghost-job detection (verifying active hiring intent) with EU Pay Transparency Directive compliance auditing (verifying mandatory salary range disclosure).

## Regulatory & Market Catalyst

- **Pay Transparency Deadline Passed:** EU Pay Transparency Directive transposition deadline passed **June 7, 2026**. Directive obligations apply to EU-facing employers regardless of local transposition speed.
- **Ghost Job Prevalence:** 18–27% of online job postings have no active hiring intent behind them (posted for brand presence or talent hoarding).
- **Market Vacuum:** Existing tools check ghost-jobs (MintCareer, jobstrack.io) or corporate pay equity (Syndio), but none combine job seeker protection with Directive disclosure auditing.

## Product Concept

1. **Active Hiring Intent Signal:** Analyzes posting duration, re-posting patterns, company headcount changes, and applicant feedback to calculate a Ghost Job Risk score.
2. **Directive Compliance Check:** Verifies presence of salary bands, gender pay gap data references, and non-discriminatory criteria.
3. **Browser Extension & API:** Provides instant trust badges on LinkedIn, Indeed, and local EU job portals.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Job seekers, career coaches, compliant recruitment platforms.
- **Pricing:** Freemium browser extension; €9.99/month pro tier for job seekers.
