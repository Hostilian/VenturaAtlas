## Executive Summary

Verifies specific workout programs, exercise claims, and fitness influencer mythologies against current peer-reviewed sports-science literature — focusing strictly on exercise claim validation rather than trainer credential lookup.

## Market Context

- **Credibility Gap:** 80% of personal training clients report not knowing what qualifications personal trainers are supposed to possess. Fitness claims on social media heavily lean on unscientific myths (hypertrophy ranges, spot reduction, supplement efficacy).
- **Positioning:** NCCA, USREPS, and NETA cover trainer credential lookup. The unfilled niche is program and claim evidence verification.

## Product Concept

1. **Claim Input & Search:** Users input a fitness claim, program structure, or workout routine.
2. **PubMed & Sports-Science Graph Lookup:** Maps claims against sports-science consensus meta-analyses.
3. **Evidence Rating:** Returns evidence level (High/Moderate/Low/Debunked) with citations.

## Buyer & Revenue Model

- **Primary Buyer:** Fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, evidence-based personal trainers.
- **Pricing:** Freemium web app; €4.99/month for unlimited claim audits.
