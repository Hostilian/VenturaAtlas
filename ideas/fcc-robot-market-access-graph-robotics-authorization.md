# FCC Robot Market-Access Graph — Foreign Mobile Robotics Authorization (idea-301)

**Score:** 80/100  |  **Category:** Robotics & Hardware Access

## Executive Summary
Regulatory origin and equipment authorization tracker for mobile robotics manufacturers navigating July 2026 FCC Covered List restrictions.

## Detailed Concept
Effective July 28, 2026, the FCC added foreign-produced advanced robotic devices to its Covered List, requiring conditional approvals for U.S. market access. FCC Robot Market-Access Graph tracks robot hardware revisions, manufacturing locations, radio modules, and conditional approval status to ensure continuous compliance for warehouse and service robots.
