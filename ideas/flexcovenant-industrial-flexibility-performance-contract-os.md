## Customer Perspective

- **Primary Customer:** Industrial energy managers and demand-response aggregators
- **Economic Buyer:** Industrial energy managers and demand-response aggregators
- **Daily User:** plant managers, utility program admins, procurement teams
- **Current Situation:** Sites rely on ad hoc spreadsheets and energy-service contracts.
- **Specific Problem:** Flexibility commitments are hard to prove and settle.
- **Frequency:** Every dispatch event and contract period.
- **Pain And Cost:** Missed revenue, disputes, and low trust in available capacity.

## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** A bankable evidence trail for industrial flexibility.
- **MVP Scope:** Commitment terms, dispatch log, performance history, settlement exports.
- **Key Features:** contract object, event ledger, exception flags, payment reconciliation
- **Tech Stack:** web app, API, database, signed event log, utility integrations

## Financial & Profitability Analysis

- **Revenue Streams:** site subscriptions, pilot fees, integrations
- **Pricing Strategy:** site fee plus volume-based settlement support
- **Gross Margin:** 70-85%
- **Break-even Point:** a handful of industrial sites with recurring dispatch

## Validation & Action Plan

- **Validation Method:** show a flexibility contract and ask if it would govern payment
- **Metric Gate:** 2 paid pilots or a signed assessment engagement
- **First Action:** interview plants with existing interruptible or DR programs
- **Seven Day Plan:** create one sample dispatch ledger
- **Thirty Day Plan:** test one site against real dispatch history
