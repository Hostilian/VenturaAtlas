When EU authorities investigate a product under the EU Forced Labour Regulation (EU 2024/1390), companies must immediately identify every SKU, factory, and transit shipment sharing the alleged subcomponent. ForcedLabor CaseGraph acts as incident response for supply chains, rapidly tracing blast-radius and assembling immutable evidence packages for regulatory defense.
