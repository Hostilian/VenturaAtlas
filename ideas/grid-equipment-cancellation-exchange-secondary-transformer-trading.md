# Grid Equipment Cancellation Exchange — Secondary Transformer & Switchgear Allocation (idea-296)

**Score:** 70.0/100  |  **Category:** Power Equipment & Energy Supply Chain

## Executive Summary
Secondary trading and allocation exchange for canceled or delayed high-voltage transformer orders and factory slot rights.

## Detailed Concept
Extreme electrical transformer lead times (up to 3-4 years) frequently bottleneck grid connections while delayed or canceled projects leave high-voltage equipment stranded. The exchange creates a verified secondary clearinghouse for electrical equipment specifications and factory slot assignments.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Launch structured pre-order or prototype offer to validate customer willingness to pay within 7 days.
