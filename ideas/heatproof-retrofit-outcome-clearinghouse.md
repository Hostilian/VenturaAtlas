# HeatProof -- Retrofit Outcome Clearinghouse

> An independent performance guarantee layer for heat-pump retrofits that compares promised efficiency against measured weather-normalized outcomes.

## Customer Perspective

- **Primary Customer:** Installers, warranty providers, housing associations
- **Economic Buyer:** Installers, warranty providers, housing associations
- **Daily User:** retrofit managers, warranty teams, building operators
- **Current Situation:** Buyers use fragmented commissioning notes and utility bills to dispute performance.
- **Specific Problem:** No neutral process checks whether the installed system delivered what was sold.
- **Frequency:** Every retrofit installation and warranty claim.
- **Pain And Cost:** Avoidable remediation, payback disputes, and confidence loss.

## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** A settlement-grade outcome statement for retrofit performance.
- **MVP Scope:** Weather normalization, metering ingest, outcome compare, cause notes.
- **Key Features:** performance statement, remediation status, dispute packet, exportable evidence trail
- **Tech Stack:** web app, analytics pipeline, meter ingest, signed reports

## Financial & Profitability Analysis

- **Revenue Streams:** per-project reviews, annual guarantee programs, insurer partnerships
- **Pricing Strategy:** fixed fee per installation outcome review
- **Gross Margin:** 75-90%
- **Break-even Point:** enough recurring projects to cover review and support labor

## Validation & Action Plan

- **Validation Method:** show a sample guarantee statement and ask who may adjudicate it
- **Metric Gate:** 2 paid pilots or 1 insurer/housing pilot
- **First Action:** interview installers and warranty stakeholders about last failed retrofit
- **Seven Day Plan:** create one realistic outcome statement
- **Thirty Day Plan:** run 3 real examples and compare to buyer judgment
