# Instant-Payment Reality Grid — VoP & Non-Euro EU Instant Payment Simulator (idea-304)

**Score:** 77/100  |  **Category:** Fintech & Payment Infrastructure

## Executive Summary
Multi-bank synthetic test network evaluating Verification of Payee (VoP) accuracy, character diacritics, and latency across non-euro EU payment providers.

## Detailed Concept
As EU non-euro Member States prepare for 2027 Instant Payments Regulation deadlines (including mandatory Verification of Payee by July 2027), payment service providers (PSPs) need real-world network testing beyond internal sandboxes. Instant-Payment Reality Grid provides synthetic multi-bank transaction testing across corporate aliases, diacritics, and edge-case account names.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Launch structured pre-order or prototype offer to validate customer willingness to pay within 7 days.
