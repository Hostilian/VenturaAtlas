## Problem Solved

## Elevator Pitch
European gamete donation is inherently cross-border, yet no individual cryobank or clinic can track global family limits or reach affected recipients when a genetic mutation is discovered. KinLedger provides a pseudonymous coordination protocol where institutions verify real-time family slot capacity and route genetic safety notifications without centralizing recipient or donor identities.
