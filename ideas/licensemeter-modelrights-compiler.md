# LicenseMeter / ModelRights Compiler — Dynamic AI Model License Telemetry Compiler Dossier

## Overview
Dynamic SCA compiler mapping open AI model licenses to real-time business telemetry (revenue, MAU, external SaaS deployment) to enforce active commercial thresholds.

## Category

## Target Customer
AI product leads, enterprise CTOs, and legal/IP counsel at fast-growing AI startups

## Problem Solved
Unexpected license non-compliance and retro-active billing when open model usage crosses un-tracked commercial revenue or MAU thresholds.

## Elevator Pitch
Open AI model licenses (Qwen, Kimi, Llama 3) increasingly embed dynamic commercial triggers—such as revenue caps ($20M/yr), MAU thresholds, or mandatory revenue-sharing agreements. Static open-source scanners can identify model licenses but fail to monitor runtime business metrics. LicenseMeter binds model license terms to live telemetry to alert legal teams before thresholds are breached.
