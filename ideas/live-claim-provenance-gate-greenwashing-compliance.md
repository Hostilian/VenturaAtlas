# Live Claim Provenance Gate — Commercial Environmental Claim Lineage & Expiry (idea-300)

**Score:** 82/100  |  **Category:** Commercial Governance & ESG

## Executive Summary
Commercial claim lineage graph that maps environmental, durability, and origin claims to underlying evidence and flags expired or scope-drifted statements across channels.

## Detailed Concept
Applying from 27 September 2026, EU Directive 2024/825 strictly prohibits unverified green claims ('eco-friendly', 'climate neutral', offset claims). Live Claim Provenance Gate builds a living evidence graph connecting supplier certificates to SKUs and marketing channels, ensuring claims automatically flag when supplier materials change or certificates expire.
