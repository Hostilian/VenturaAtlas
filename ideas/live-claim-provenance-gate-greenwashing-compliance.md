## Executive Summary
Commercial claim lineage graph that maps environmental, durability, and origin claims to underlying evidence and flags expired or scope-drifted statements across channels.

## Detailed Concept
Applying through Member State measures from 27 September 2026, Directive (EU) 2024/825 prohibits specified greenwashing practices, including unsupported generic environmental claims, whole-product scope overstatement, and offset-based neutral/reduced/positive product-impact claims. Live Claim Provenance Gate links supplier evidence to SKUs and marketing channels so claims can be flagged when evidence expires or scope drifts.

Legal correction: Directive 2024/825 does not simply prohibit every “unverified green claim.” It creates several specific unfair-practice rules, including generic claims without demonstrable recognised excellent environmental performance, whole-product/business scope overstatement, and offset-based neutral/reduced/positive product-impact claims. The separate proposed Green Claims Directive is stalled and currently imposes no operative third-party pre-verification duty.
