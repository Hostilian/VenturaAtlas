# Machine Safety State Drift Gate — Machinery Regulation 2027 Revalidation Engine (idea-299)

**Score:** 84/100  |  **Category:** Industrial IoT & Safety Engineering

## Executive Summary
Cross-vendor industrial software and firmware fingerprinting engine that detects silent safety-state drift across PLC, drive, and robot configurations.

## Detailed Concept
The EU Machinery Regulation (applying Jan 2027) brings software updates, AI safety functions, and cybersecurity under formal machinery safety rules. Machine Safety State Drift Gate captures verified baseline fingerprints of PLCs, drives, and sensors, triggering automated revalidation workflows whenever firmware or safety parameter updates alter the validated safety case.
