## Customer Perspective

- **Primary Customer:** Online shoppers buying furniture, bags, cases, mounting hardware, replacement parts, or any product where exact fit matters
- **Economic Buyer:** The buyer who posts the measurement bounty
- **Daily User:** Buyer (requests measurement), owner (provides ruler-in-frame evidence), reviewer (validates accuracy)
- **Customer Type:** B2B, B2C, or marketplace participant depending on segment
- **Current Situation:** Customers currently rely on search engines, unverified reviews, manual research, or internal scripts.
- **Specific Problem:** Catalog exterior dimensions are insufficient for purchase decisions where internal clearance, usable depth, or soft-material behavior determines fit.
- **Frequency:** High for high-consideration purchases; multiple times per year for active shoppers
- **Pain And Cost:** Product return costs, wrong purchases, lost research time

### Current Alternatives
- Retailer spec sheets (exterior only)
- Forum posts (often wrong or outdated)
- Contacting sellers (slow, informal)
- AR measurement tools (need physical access to item)

## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** A paid, public, and compounding graph of exact usable dimensions — internal bag widths, mounting-hole distances, door clearances, connector positions — funded by buyers who need specific measurements before purchasing.
- **MVP Scope:** A bounty marketplace for ruler-in-frame visual measurements. Buyer specifies start and end points; owner follows a guided measurement template; result is verified and linked to the product model ID for reuse.
- **Key Features:** Escrow payment; Challenge-code verification; Evidence repository
- **Tech Stack:** Vision AI pre-screening; OCR label extraction; Product graph matching

## Financial & Profitability Analysis

- **Revenue Streams:** Platform fee on each measurement bounty. Unlock fee for reusable results. Affiliate links from "Will it fit?" content. Long-term: API for design tools, retailers, and AR applications.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Take rate on bounty escrow (15-20%) + Unlock fee (€1-€2)
- **Gross Margin:** 60–80% after scale
- **Break-even Point:** 8-34 paid requests per month

## Validation & Action Plan
