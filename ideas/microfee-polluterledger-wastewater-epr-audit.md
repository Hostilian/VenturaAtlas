# MicroFee / PolluterLedger — Urban Wastewater EPR Fee Scenario & Audit Engine Dossier

## Overview
Chemical hazard & volume scenario engine calculating extended producer responsibility (EPR) liabilities for pharmaceutical and cosmetic manufacturers under EU wastewater rules.

## Target Customer
Pharma CFOs, cosmetics brand operations leads, and environmental compliance consultants.

## Problem Solved
Complex multi-SKU chemical hazard calculations and uncertain EPR fee allocations across 27 EU member state wastewater treatment regimes.
