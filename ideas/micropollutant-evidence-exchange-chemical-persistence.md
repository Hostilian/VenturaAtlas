# Micropollutant Evidence Exchange — Chemical Persistence & Degradability Ledger Dossier

## Overview
Shared, peer-audited evidence exchange for chemical substance persistence, bio-degradability, and aquatic toxicity data used in environmental fee audits.

## Target Customer
Chemical manufacturers, pharma regulatory affairs, and environmental consultancy firms.

## Problem Solved
Redundant, expensive lab testing by individual chemical suppliers seeking to prove lower environmental persistence for EPR fee reductions.
