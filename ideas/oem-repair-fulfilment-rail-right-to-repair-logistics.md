# OEM Repair Fulfilment Rail — Right to Repair After-Sales Logistics Engine (idea-295)

**Score:** 72.0/100  |  **Category:** Circular Economy & After-Sales Logistics

## Executive Summary
End-to-end repair fulfillment and spare parts dispatch rail for smaller appliance and electronics OEMs under EU Right-to-Repair mandates.

## Detailed Concept
EU Right-to-Repair regulations obligate hardware brands to offer spare parts and repair services at reasonable prices. OEM Repair Fulfilment Rail connects hardware OEMs to a network of independent repair technicians, local parts hubs, and automated customer return logistics.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Launch structured pre-order or prototype offer to validate customer willingness to pay within 7 days.
