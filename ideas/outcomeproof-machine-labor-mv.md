# OutcomeProof — Machine Labor M&V Dossier

## Overview
Independent Measurement & Verification (M&V) engine validating outcome-priced AI agent invoicing against buyer system-of-record state.

## Target Customer
Enterprise procurement directors, FinOps teams, and buyers of outcome-priced AI agent services.

## Problem Solved
AI vendors billing for claimed outcomes (resolutions, qualifications) that fail downstream ERP posting, control matrices, or maturation windows.
