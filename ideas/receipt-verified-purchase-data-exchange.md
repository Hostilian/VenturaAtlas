## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** A privacy-first marketplace where consumers monetize their anonymized, receipt-verified purchase data — enabling retailers and brands to acquire accurate, consent-based purchase intelligence.
- **MVP Scope:** A consent-based, privacy-first receipt data exchange with local processing, explicit purpose limitation, and consumer payment for data sharing. Explore DRP (Digital Receipt Protocol) as the technical standard.
- **Key Features:** Escrow payment; Challenge-code verification; Evidence repository
