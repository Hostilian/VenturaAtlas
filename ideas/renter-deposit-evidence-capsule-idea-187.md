# Renter Deposit Evidence Capsule (idea-187)

**Score:** 8.34/10  |  **Category:** Consumer-Owned Proof & Evidence

## Executive Summary
Tenant-owned mobile web product guiding renters through room-by-room move-in and move-out condition capturing.

## Product Description
Produces a timestamped chronological PDF report and ZIP package containing original evidence, hashes, and inventory comparison independent of landlord accounts.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Offer standardized $15-$49 report/package. Target 3 paid pre-orders within 7 days.
