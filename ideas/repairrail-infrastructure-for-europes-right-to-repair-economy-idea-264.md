# RepairRail — Infrastructure for Europe's Right-to-Repair Economy (idea-264)

**Score:** 8.8/10  |  **Category:** Right to Repair & Circular Economy

## Executive Summary
B2B clearing network and API connecting retailers, OEMs, repairers, and parts distributors under EU Right-to-Repair regulations.

## Product Description
An end-to-end repair clearing network enabling retailers and manufacturers to offer instant 1-click device repair, parts routing, and status tracking.
