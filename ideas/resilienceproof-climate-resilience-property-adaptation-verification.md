# ResilienceProof — Climate Resilience & Property Adaptation Verification Rail (idea-290)

**Score:** 82.0/100  |  **Category:** Climate Resilience & Property Insurance

## Executive Summary
Verification rail proving flood, wildfire, and climate resilience property retrofits to unlock lower insurance premiums and mortgage rates.

## Detailed Concept
As the UK Flood Performance Certificate (FPC) framework launches, ResilienceProof captures geotagged installation evidence, product certificates, and maintenance records for property adaptation retrofits. Following climate events, actual loss outcomes are linked back to verified retrofits to validate insurer loss-reduction models.
