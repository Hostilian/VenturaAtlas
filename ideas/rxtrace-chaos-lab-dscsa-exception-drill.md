# RxTrace Chaos Lab — DSCSA Exception Drill & Physical/Data Reconciliation Engine (idea-310)

**Score:** 83/100  |  **Category:** Pharmaceutical Supply Chain

## Executive Summary
Synthetic package and barcode exception testing harness for independent pharmacies preparing for the FDA DSCSA November 2026 small-dispenser deadline.

## Detailed Concept
With the FDA's DSCSA small-dispenser exemption expiring 27 November 2026, pharmacies face operational chaos from EPCIS data mismatches, unreadable barcodes, and physical-versus-electronic discrepancies. RxTrace Chaos Lab injects controlled synthetic test cases to verify scanner workflows, quarantine isolation, and exception evidence logging.
