# Second-Life Product Passport (idea-188)

**Score:** 8.08/10  |  **Category:** Consumer-Owned Proof & Evidence

## Executive Summary
Consumer-owned transferable history and repair record for durable goods lacking native manufacturer passports.

## Product Description
Records model, serial numbers, ownership proof, service events, battery health, and warranty status for e-bikes, camera gear, and high-value equipment.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Offer standardized $15-$49 report/package. Target 3 paid pre-orders within 7 days.
