# Subscription Rights Chaos Lab — Recurring Payment State-Machine QA (idea-306)

**Score:** 75/100  |  **Category:** Consumer Protection & Billing Tech

## Executive Summary
Automated lifecycle test suite for subscription platforms to verify renewal notices, 14-day cooling-off refunds, and one-click cancellation flows.

## Detailed Concept
Ahead of the UK's Spring 2027 subscription contract reforms (mandating clear pre-contract terms, mandatory renewal reminders, cooling-off rights, and easy online cancellation), Subscription Rights Chaos Lab executes end-to-end synthetic subscriber lifecycles to detect billing state-machine bugs and notice delivery failures.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Launch structured pre-order or prototype offer to validate customer willingness to pay within 7 days.
