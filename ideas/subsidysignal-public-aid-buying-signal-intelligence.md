# SubsidySignal -- Public Aid Buying-Signal Intelligence

> **OMEGA Round**: OMEGA XVII | **Research date**: 2026-08-17 | **Score**: 81.0/100 | **Status**: priority

## One-sentence concept

Weekly curated alerts translating newly public EU de minimis aid events into commercially relevant buying signals for B2B suppliers -- one country, one vertical, specific declared funding purpose.

## Target customer

B2B industrial automation integrators, ERP vendors, industrial software suppliers targeting manufacturing or capital-intensive verticals

## Problem solved

B2B suppliers lack early signals of company investment intent before procurement events become publicly visible.
