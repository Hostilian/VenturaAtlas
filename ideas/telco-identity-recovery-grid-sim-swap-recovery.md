# Telco Identity Recovery Grid — SIM-Swap & Account Migration Recovery Tester (idea-312)

**Score:** 79/100  |  **Category:** Telecom & Security Ops

## Executive Summary
Adversarial customer-care workflow test grid for telecommunication carriers to verify SIM replacement, eSIM migration, and account recovery security.

## Detailed Concept
While carriers deploy real-time SIM-swap APIs, customer recovery workflows remain vulnerable to social engineering and session retention bugs. Telco Identity Recovery Grid executes authorized synthetic takeover and recovery journeys (PIN resets, eSIM transfers, port freezes) to ensure compromised lines are reliably secured.
