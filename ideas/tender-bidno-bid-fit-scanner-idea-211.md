# Tender Bid/No-Bid Fit Scanner (idea-211)

**Score:** 7.71/10  |  **Category:** Independence & Operational Trust

## Executive Summary
Procurement requirement scanner evaluating company fit, evidence gaps, and disqualifying conditions before committing to public tenders.

## Product Description
Maps mandatory requirements against team experience and capacity to prevent costly bid efforts on low-probability tenders.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Offer standardized $49-$99 diagnostic report. Target 3 paid pilots within 7 days.
