# Trusted Product Label Change Watch (idea-255)

**Score:** 7.63/10  |  **Category:** Packaged food

## Executive Summary
A family watchlist that compares user-photographed ingredient and allergen labels over time and alerts to visible changes while treating packaging as the final source.

## Product Description
Household ingredient and allergen change-detection engine comparing timestamped label photographs across packaging versions to highlight unexpected reformulations.
