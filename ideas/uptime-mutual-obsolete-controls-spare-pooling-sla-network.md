# Uptime Mutual — Obsolete Controls Spare Pooling & SLA Guarantee Network (idea-292)

**Score:** 80.0/100  |  **Category:** Industrial Maintenance & Spare Parts

## Executive Summary
Guaranteed restoration-time mutual pooling customer-owned spares, remanufactured inventory, and cross-compatible components for aging factory lines.

## Detailed Concept
Instead of selling obsolete MRO parts, Uptime Mutual sells uptime SLAs. By normalizing fragmented spare inventories across participating factories, repair houses, and remanufacturers, the network dispatches compatible replacement components within 12 to 48 hours without forcing plants to hold expensive redundant stock.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Launch structured pre-order or prototype offer to validate customer willingness to pay within 7 days.
