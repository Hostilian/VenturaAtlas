# UrbanMine Futures — Strategic Raw Material Feedstock Forecasting Engine Dossier

## Overview
Above-ground mineral exploration terminal forecasting end-of-life permanent magnet and rare-earth feedstock locations from decommissioned wind turbines and EVs.

## Target Customer
Rare-earth recycling plant operators, scrap traders, magnet manufacturers, and battery recyclers.

## Problem Solved
Lack of commercial visibility into future geographic locations and volumes of recyclable NdFeB magnets needed to justify recycling plant investments.
