# Vendor Payment Change Challenge (idea-209)

**Score:** 7.95/10  |  **Category:** Independence & Operational Trust

## Executive Summary
Lightweight verification procedure for small businesses receiving supplier requests to change bank routing or payment details.

## Product Description
Freezes payment change requests, enforces out-of-band callback challenges, and produces auditable payment-change evidence receipts.

## 7-Day Payment Experiment
Offer standardized $49-$99 diagnostic report. Target 3 paid pilots within 7 days.
