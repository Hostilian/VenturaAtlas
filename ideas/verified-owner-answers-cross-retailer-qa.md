## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** Receipt-verified past buyers earn a small bounty for answering product questions with short, evidence-backed video answers — providing the cross-retailer, buyer-funded verified Q&A that Amazon Answers and Social Ask have not.
- **MVP Scope:** A cross-retailer Q&A platform where buyers post a bounty for receipt-verified past owners to provide short, specific, video-evidenced answers. Answers link to product model IDs for portability.
- **Key Features:** Escrow payment; Challenge-code verification; Evidence repository
- **Tech Stack:** Vision AI pre-screening; OCR label extraction; Product graph matching

## Financial & Profitability Analysis

- **Revenue Streams:** Platform fee on bounty. Future: answer royalties when reused; retailer API for verified answer data.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Take rate on bounty escrow (15-20%) + Unlock fee (€1-€2)
- **Gross Margin:** 60–80% after scale
- **Break-even Point:** 8-34 paid requests per month
