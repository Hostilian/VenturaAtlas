# Wastewater EPR Settlement Rail — Pharma & Cosmetics Micropollutant Clearing Network (idea-293)

**Score:** 78.0/100  |  **Category:** Environmental Compliance & Clearing

## Executive Summary
Neutral settlement rail calculating, verifying, and reconciling multi-party Extended Producer Responsibility payments from pharma/cosmetics producers to wastewater utilities under EU rules.

## Detailed Concept
The revised EU UWWTD requires pharmaceutical and cosmetics manufacturers to finance quaternary wastewater treatment by 2028. The settlement rail ingests producer volume data, product assessment bases, and plant treatment invoices to automate multi-party fee allocations, dispute workflows, and financial clearing.
