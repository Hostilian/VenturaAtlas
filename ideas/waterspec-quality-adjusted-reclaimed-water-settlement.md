## Customer Perspective

- **Primary Customer:** Municipal reuse utilities and industrial water buyers
- **Economic Buyer:** Municipal reuse utilities and industrial water buyers
- **Daily User:** utility operators, procurement teams, industrial facility managers
- **Current Situation:** Teams use commodity-style contracts and manual quality checks.
- **Specific Problem:** Exact water quality and reliability need a better contract object.
- **Frequency:** Every delivery period and contract term.
- **Pain And Cost:** Service interruptions, quality disputes, and poor project bankability.

## Product Definition

- **Core Proposition:** Quality-adjusted settlement for reclaimed water.
- **MVP Scope:** quality bands, delivery logs, reliability metrics, make-good statements
- **Key Features:** settlement sheet, evidence trail, exception flags, contract export
- **Tech Stack:** web app, ingestion pipeline, database, signed reports

## Financial & Profitability Analysis

- **Revenue Streams:** per-project reviews, recurring utility contracts, financing support
- **Pricing Strategy:** contract-size based fee plus reconciliation support
- **Gross Margin:** 70-90%
- **Break-even Point:** a few reusable utility contracts

## Validation & Action Plan

- **Validation Method:** ask buyers if they would enforce a quality-adjusted sheet
- **Metric Gate:** 1-2 paid pilots or a financing conversation
- **First Action:** interview municipal reuse teams and industrial water users
- **Seven Day Plan:** create one settlement sheet with realistic spec bands
- **Thirty Day Plan:** review a live or representative reuse project
