# Launch Plan — Wastewater EPR Settlement Rail — Pharma & Cosmetics Micropollutant Clearing Network

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** National Producer Responsibility Organisations (PROs), pharma/cosmetic producers, and wastewater utilities
- **ICP:** National Producer Responsibility Organisations (PROs), pharma/cosmetic producers, and wastewater utilities
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Complex multi-party cost allocation and reconciliation for mandatory micropollutant treatment funding.
- **Value Proposition:** Ensures legal compliance, transparent cost allocation, and dispute-free statutory settlements.

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Contact 20 targeted prospects with an immediate pilot offer
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
