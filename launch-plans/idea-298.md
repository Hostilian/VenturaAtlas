# Launch Plan — SKU Resurrection Lab — Marketplace Product Safety & Anti-Reappearance QA

- **Initial Niche:** E-commerce marketplaces, multi-vendor platforms, and refurbished device sites
- **ICP:** E-commerce marketplaces, multi-vendor platforms, and refurbished device sites
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Recalled or banned unsafe products sneaking back onto marketplace platforms through listing mutations
