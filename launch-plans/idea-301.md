# Launch Plan — FCC Robot Market-Access Graph — Foreign Mobile Robotics Authorization

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Mobile robot OEMs, importers, distributors, and telecommunications certification bodies
- **ICP:** Mobile robot OEMs, importers, distributors, and telecommunications certification bodies
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Uncertainty surrounding FCC Covered List restrictions on foreign-produced mobile robots

### First 100 Customers

- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
