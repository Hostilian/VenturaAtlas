# Launch Plan — ControlRight — Contract-to-Land-Registry Preflight & Event Clock

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** UK conveyancing law firms, property developers, and land agents
- **ICP:** UK conveyancing law firms, property developers, and land agents
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Manual data extraction errors and missed 60-day filing deadlines for HMLR land-control filings
