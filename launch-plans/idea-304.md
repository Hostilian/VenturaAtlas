# Launch Plan — Instant-Payment Reality Grid — VoP & Non-Euro EU Instant Payment Simulator

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Non-euro European PSPs, challenger banks, and payment gateways in Czechia, Poland, Hungary, Romania
- **ICP:** Non-euro European PSPs, challenger banks, and payment gateways in Czechia, Poland, Hungary, Romania
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Verification of Payee (VoP) failures caused by accented names, corporate aliases, and bank mismatch rules
- **Value Proposition:** Meet mandatory ECB and EU Instant Payments Regulation 2027 VoP compliance milestones

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Interview payment engineering leads at 10 Central European regional banks
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
