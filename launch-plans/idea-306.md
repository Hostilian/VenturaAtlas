# Launch Plan — Subscription Rights Chaos Lab — Recurring Payment State-Machine QA

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** SaaS platforms, media subscription businesses, and e-commerce box services
- **ICP:** SaaS platforms, media subscription businesses, and e-commerce box services
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Silent failures in subscription renewal reminder emails and online cancellation state transitions
- **Value Proposition:** Comply with UK Spring 2027 subscription law and reduce customer support chargebacks

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Test cancellation and notice flows for 10 UK subscription SaaS platforms
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.

- [ ] Finalize target prospect list of 30 names.

- [ ] Conduct first 10 outreach attempts.
