# Launch Plan — PA RealityGrid — Synthetic-Patient Failure Laboratory for Prior Authorization

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Healthtech integration vendors, EHR providers, clearinghouses, and health system interoperability teams
- **ICP:** Healthtech integration vendors, EHR providers, clearinghouses, and health system interoperability teams
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Silent administrative failures and state mismatches in prior-authorization API workflows delaying patient care
- **Value Proposition:** Comply with CMS 2027 API rules, prevent claim rejections, and protect patient care timelines

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
