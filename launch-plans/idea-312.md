# Launch Plan — Telco Identity Recovery Grid — SIM-Swap & Account Migration Recovery Tester

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Telecom carrier security teams, MVNO operations leads, and identity verification partners
- **ICP:** Telecom carrier security teams, MVNO operations leads, and identity verification partners
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Account recovery process failures allowing attackers to retain access after SIM-swap discovery
