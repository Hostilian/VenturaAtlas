# Launch Plan — PA Denial Semantics Observatory — Payer Authorization Metrics & Transparency Index

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Health system revenue cycle directors, medical specialty societies, and health policy researchers
- **ICP:** Health system revenue cycle directors, medical specialty societies, and health policy researchers
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Fragmented, opaque payer prior-authorization metrics preventing data-driven payer negotiations
- **Value Proposition:** Benchmark payer turnaround times and identify high-overturn procedure categories for appeals

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Publish a free 2026 Payer Prior Authorization Benchmark Report to 50 health system RCM leads
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
