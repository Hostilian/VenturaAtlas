# Launch Plan — LicenseMeter / ModelRights Compiler — Dynamic AI Model License Telemetry Compiler

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** AI product leads, enterprise CTOs, and legal/IP counsel at fast-growing AI startups
- **ICP:** AI product leads, enterprise CTOs, and legal/IP counsel at fast-growing AI startups
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Unexpected license non-compliance and retro-active billing when open model usage crosses un-tracked commercial revenue or MAU thresholds.
- **Value Proposition:** Avoid breach-of-contract lawsuits and unexpected licensing fees from major open-model AI providers.

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
