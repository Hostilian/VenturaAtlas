# Launch Plan — Donor VariantWatch — Post-Donation Genomic Surveillance Engine

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Cryobank medical directors, clinical geneticists, and reproductive health labs
- **ICP:** Cryobank medical directors, clinical geneticists, and reproductive health labs
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for Undetected genetic risk when donor screening interpretations change years after gamete distribution.
- **Value Proposition:** Fulfill mandatory SoHO biovigilance obligations and prevent inherited disease transmission.

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Contact 20 targeted prospects with an immediate pilot offer
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.

- [ ] Finalize target prospect list of 30 names.
