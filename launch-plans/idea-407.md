# Launch Plan — AidGraph -- Public Money Graph Platform

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Grant consultants, subsidy advisers, and B2B sales intelligence teams
- **ICP:** Grant consultants, subsidy advisers, and B2B sales intelligence teams
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for EU de minimis aid registers create a new stream of observable company funding events, but the underlying corporate group graph is fragmented across jurisdictions.
- **Value Proposition:** Group-resolution step confirmed painful by EU Council feedback and Czechia August 2026 bulk-upload register feature.

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Sell 3 prepaid AidHeadroom EUR99 audits to grant consultants before writing any code.
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.
