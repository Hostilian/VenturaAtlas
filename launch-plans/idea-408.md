# Launch Plan — AidHeadroom -- De Minimis Group Headroom Audit

## Go-to-Market

- **Initial Niche:** Independent EU grant consultants managing applications for corporate clients
- **ICP:** Independent EU grant consultants managing applications for corporate clients
- **Positioning:** A specialized, fast, low-friction solution for The de minimis ceiling applies to the entire corporate group as one undertaking. Assembling the group perimeter, historical awards, and new 2026 register entries into a defensible evidence trail is genuinely painful.
- **Value Proposition:** A EUR100k-EUR250k grant application makes a EUR99 evidence audit negligible. Omission risks aid repayment or ineligibility.

### First 10 Customers
- Founder-led direct outreach to 50 targeted prospects.
- Local community and industry forum engagement.
- Partner referrals from non-competing service providers.

### First 100 Customers
- Targeted outbound campaigns based on public trigger signals.
- Word-of-mouth referral incentives for existing customers.
- Search-optimized case studies and solution guides.

### Sales Channels
- Direct cold email / LinkedIn outreach
- Domain-specific directories and listings
- Strategic partnerships with ecosystem platforms

## Actions

- **First Action:** Send cold message to 30-40 EU grant consultants offering De Minimis Group Headroom Audit EUR99. Require 3 prepaid audits as PASS.
- **First Measurement:** Number of positive replies and paid commitments.
- **First Delivery:** Manual or semi-automated delivery of the first outcome.

### 7-Day Checklist
- [ ] Finalize target prospect list of 30 names.
- [ ] Prepare cold outreach template and offer one-pager.
- [ ] Conduct first 10 outreach attempts.
- [ ] Track responses, objections, and conversation notes.
