  [IDEA]       = Your idea name or brief description

========================================================================
STAGE 1: DISCOVERY — FINDING IDEAS
========================================================================

--- PROMPT 1.1: UNDERSERVED MARKET SCAN ---
I'm looking for underserved business opportunities in [MARKET] as of 2025-2026.

Please analyze:
1. What regulatory changes in the past 18 months have created new compliance tool needs?
2. What new AI/LLM capabilities are now available that weren't 2 years ago?
3. What previously manual workflows are now ripe for automation?
4. What consumer behavior shifts have created new demand that existing products don't serve?

For each opportunity you identify, provide:
- Problem statement (who suffers, what pain, how often)
- Existing solutions and their gaps
- Rough market size estimate
- Initial startup cost estimate
- Speed to first revenue


--- PROMPT 1.2: ADJACENT IDEA GENERATOR ---
I'm exploring the business idea: [IDEA]

Generate 10 adjacent business ideas that:
1. Serve the same customer but solve a different problem
2. Solve the same problem for a different customer segment
3. Use the same core technology in a different market
4. Are upstream or downstream in the same value chain
5. Are the B2B version of this B2C idea (or vice versa)

For each adjacent idea, rate:
- Differentiation from original (1-10)
- Market size potential (1-10)
- Ease of validation (1-10)


--- PROMPT 1.3: COMPETITOR GAP ANALYSIS ---
For the business idea: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]

Please identify:
1. The top 5 existing solutions or competitors in this space
2. For each competitor: pricing, key features, key weaknesses, customer complaints
3. The whitespace — what specific use cases do ALL competitors fail to serve well?
4. A "wedge" — the narrowest version of this idea that could beat all existing solutions

Conclude with: Is this market worth disrupting? Why?


--- PROMPT 1.4: TREND ALIGNMENT CHECK ---
Business idea: [IDEA]

Assess alignment with 2025-2026 trends:
1. AI agent adoption (businesses actively paying for autonomous AI workflows?)
2. EU regulatory expansion (GDPR, DSA, DMA, DAC7, AI Act — does this idea benefit?)
3. Post-pandemic behavior shifts (remote work, e-commerce, health)
4. Developer-led growth (can this support a PLG / developer-community motion?)
5. Bootstrap / indie hacker movement (can this work as a solo founder business?)

Rate each trend: Strong / Moderate / Weak
Overall trend score: X/10


========================================================================
STAGE 2: VALIDATION — TESTING ASSUMPTIONS
========================================================================

--- PROMPT 2.1: ASSUMPTION MAPPING ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]

List ALL assumptions this idea makes that could be wrong:
1. Market assumptions (does the problem exist at scale?)
2. Customer assumptions (will the target customer pay for a solution?)
3. Technical assumptions (can this be built at the assumed cost?)
4. Distribution assumptions (can we reach customers through the assumed channel?)
5. Economic assumptions (will unit economics work at scale?)
6. Regulatory assumptions (is this legally permissible in [MARKET]?)
7. Timing assumptions (is the market ready for this now?)

For each assumption:
- How likely is it to be true? (1-10)
- How easy is it to test? (1-10)
- How critical is it to the business? (1-10)

Prioritize the 3 most important assumptions to test first.


--- PROMPT 2.2: CUSTOMER DISCOVERY INTERVIEW SCRIPT ---
I'm building: [IDEA]
My target customer is: [CUSTOMER]

Write a 15-question customer discovery interview script to validate whether
this problem is real and worth solving.

The script should:
1. Start with open-ended questions about the customer's current workflow
2. NOT lead the witness — never mention our solution
3. Explore frequency, severity, and cost of the problem
4. Understand what they currently do to solve it
5. Discover what would make them switch
6. End with a pull-forward question to gauge intent

Include recommended follow-up probes for each question.


--- PROMPT 2.3: LANDING PAGE VALIDATION BRIEF ---
I want to test market demand for: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]

Write:
1. A compelling headline (under 10 words) capturing the core value proposition
2. Three supporting subheadlines addressing the top 3 customer pains
3. A "How it works" 3-step description
4. Three social proof angles — what would make someone trust this?
5. A call-to-action that gets early sign-ups or waitlist registrations
6. Five objections a visitor might have and how to address them
7. A/B test ideas: 2 alternative headlines to test


--- PROMPT 2.4: DEMAND SIGNAL RESEARCH ---
I'm researching demand for: [IDEA]
The problem it solves: [PROBLEM]

Help me find demand signals:
1. 10 specific subreddits, Facebook groups, or Slack communities where
   the target customer would discuss this problem
2. 5 search queries to find complaints on Reddit, Twitter/X, G2, Capterra
3. 3 relevant job titles that experience this problem — and what job boards
   to search for volume
4. 3 ways to do a "smoke test" to validate demand before building anything
5. A cold outreach message to get 10 customer discovery calls in 48 hours


--- PROMPT 2.5: KILL CRITERIA CHECKLIST ---
Business idea: [IDEA]

Create a kill criteria checklist — specific, measurable conditions that
would tell me this idea is NOT worth pursuing.

Format:
KILL CONDITION: [specific observable fact]
HOW TO TEST: [specific action to confirm/deny within 72 hours]
KILL IF: [threshold — e.g., "less than 3 of 20 people agree this is their top pain"]

Create 8 kill conditions covering:
1. Problem severity (is it painful enough?)
2. Market size (is it big enough?)
3. Willingness to pay (will they actually pay?)
4. Switching costs (are they too locked in to alternatives?)
5. Technical feasibility (can we build a useful v1 in under 60 days?)
6. Distribution (is there a reachable channel?)
7. Competition (dominant incumbent with deep moat?)
8. Founder fit (do we have an edge?)

--- PROMPT 3.1: REVENUE MODEL DESIGN ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Market: [MARKET]

Design 3 alternative revenue models:

MODEL A: Subscription (SaaS)
- Monthly price point options (3 tiers)
- Rationale for each tier
- Churn assumptions
- LTV:CAC target

MODEL B: Transaction / Usage-Based
- Unit of pricing
- Price per unit
- Volume assumptions

MODEL C: One-Time or License
- Price point
- Target deal size
- Renewal/expansion path

For each: Year 1 revenue projection (conservative/base/optimistic),
minimum customers to $10k MRR, pros and cons.


--- PROMPT 3.2: STARTUP COST BREAKDOWN ---
I want to launch: [IDEA]
My technical background: [YOUR SKILLS]
Timeline to launch: [X weeks/months]

Create a detailed startup cost breakdown:
1. One-time costs (domain, design, legal entity formation)
2. Monthly recurring costs (hosting, SaaS tools, APIs, marketing)
3. Time cost (hours per week required)
4. First 90 days total cost estimate
5. "Zero-dollar" version — minimum viable start with $0?
6. "Recommended" version — what $500-$2,000 buys?
7. "Proper" version — what $5,000-$10,000 buys?

Include specific tool recommendations at each tier.


--- PROMPT 3.3: UNIT ECONOMICS MODEL ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Revenue model: [SUBSCRIPTION / TRANSACTION / OTHER]
Pricing: $[X] per [month/unit/project]

Calculate unit economics:
1. Gross margin estimate (% of revenue = gross profit)
2. Customer Acquisition Cost estimate for 3 channels
3. Customer Lifetime Value estimate at X% monthly churn
4. Payback period in months
5. LTV:CAC ratio for each channel
6. Break-even: number of customers to cover $[Y] fixed monthly costs

Flag healthy vs. unhealthy unit economics scenarios.


--- PROMPT 3.4: FINANCIAL SCENARIO PLANNING ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Revenue model: [SUBSCRIPTION]
Target price: $[X]/month

Create 3 financial scenarios for Year 1:
CONSERVATIVE: [growth rate], [churn rate], total revenue, end-of-year MRR
BASE: [growth rate], [churn rate], total revenue, end-of-year MRR
OPTIMISTIC: [growth rate], [churn rate], total revenue, end-of-year MRR

For the BASE scenario:
- Monthly P&L for months 1-12
- Cumulative cash position chart
- When does the business become cash flow positive?

--- PROMPT 4.1: FIRST CUSTOMER ACQUISITION PLAN ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Market: [MARKET]

I need my first 10 paying customers:

DAY 1-7:
- Where exactly will I find these customers? (3 specific channels)
- What will I say in outreach? (write a DM/email template)
- Goal for first conversation (learn, not sell)
- Outreach attempts per day

DAY 8-14:
- How to convert interested prospects to paying customers
- What to offer first customers (pricing, guarantee, service level)
- 5 key questions to ask in the sales conversation

BY DAY 30:
- Customer milestone
- Revenue milestone
- Learning milestone

What is the single highest-leverage action in the next 24 hours?


--- PROMPT 4.2: GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY ---
Business idea: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Launch budget: $[X]

Design a 90-day GTM strategy:

PHASE 1 (Days 1-30): Foundation
- Brand and positioning: tagline, value proposition
- Minimum viable product scope
- Initial channel (pick ONE, go deep)
- Content strategy for target customer attraction

PHASE 2 (Days 31-60): Validation
- Second channel expansion
- Referral/word-of-mouth mechanics
- Customer success rituals

PHASE 3 (Days 61-90): Scale
- Paid acquisition test
- Partnership/integration opportunities
- Case study/social proof development

Metrics targets at 30/60/90 days: revenue, customers, satisfaction.


--- PROMPT 4.3: LAUNCH ANNOUNCEMENT PACK ---
I'm launching: [IDEA]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]

Write my complete launch pack:
1. Product Hunt description (260 char tagline + body)
2. Twitter/X launch thread (5 tweets)

5. Hacker News "Show HN" post (honest, technical)

--- PROMPT 5.1: CUSTOMER FEEDBACK ANALYSIS ---
I've collected customer feedback for [IDEA].

Here is the raw feedback:
[PASTE FEEDBACK HERE]

Analyze this feedback:
1. Group into themes (most frequent pain points)
2. What customers LOVE (never touch these)

5. Signal vs. noise (best customers vs. bad-fit customers)
6. Top 3 product changes in the next 30 days

--- PROMPT 5.2: PIVOT TRIGGER ANALYSIS ---
My business [IDEA] has been running for [X months].

Current metrics:
- MRR: $[X]
- Customer count: [X]
- Churn: [X]% per month
- CAC: $[X]
- NPS: [X]

Help me think through whether to pivot:
1. Is this a "growth problem" or a "thesis problem"?
2. What would the data look like at [6 months] on a good trajectory?
3. What metric is most concerning? What does it signal?
4. Top 3 pivot options (what changes each time)
5. Minimum experiment to test each pivot direction in 2 weeks
6. How to decide: stick, pivot, or kill?


--- PROMPT 5.3: GROWTH EXPERIMENT DESIGN ---
Business: [IDEA]
Current MRR: $[X]
Goal: grow from $[X] to $[Y] in [X] months

Design a growth experiment framework:

1. Identify the biggest constraint:
   - Awareness (not enough people know we exist)
   - Conversion (people see us but don't sign up)
   - Activation (sign up but don't get value)
   - Retention (get value but churn)
   - Revenue (stay but don't expand)

2. For the biggest constraint, propose 5 experiments:
   - Hypothesis (belief + expected outcome)
   - Effort (Low/Medium/High)
   - Expected impact (Low/Medium/High)
   - Measurement metric
   - Test duration (days)

3. Rank by effort-to-impact ratio. Which runs first?
