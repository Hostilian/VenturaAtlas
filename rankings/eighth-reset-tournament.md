## Tournament Decision Rationale

### Why FactBounty Won

FactBounty scored highest because it combines the broadest addressable use case (any objective product question) with the fastest path to first revenue (1–7 days with no pre-funded worker pool) and the lowest startup cost (€0–€35). The buyer-pays-before-responder-works mechanic eliminates inventory risk. The compounding fact graph creates a defensible data advantage that competitors cannot easily replicate.

**Decisive factors:**
- fastestPathToRevenue: 94/100 — first paid request possible within 7 days
- lowestCostLaunch: 96/100 — €0–€35 to first transaction
- bestConsumerOpportunity: 82/100 — direct buyer demand validated by adjacent markets
- bestMarketplaceOpportunity: 85/100 — two-sided network with compounding reuse value

### Why MeasureGraph Was Runner-Up

MeasureGraph scored second because it is the narrowest, safest, and most legally defensible variant of the FactBounty concept. The ruler-in-frame measurement is the simplest, least disputable evidence type. It is the most likely starting wedge for a solo founder who wants to minimize fraud, dispute, and liability risk.

**Distinguishing factors:**
- soloFounderPotential: 90/100 — simplest mechanics; fewer edge cases
- evidenceQuality: 9.5/10 — objective measurements are easy to verify and hard to fake
- regulatorySimplicity: 7.7/10 — measurement facts carry lower liability than subjective compatibility claims

### Why Product Evidence API Ranked Third Overall

The Product Evidence API scored highest on overallOpportunity (85.7) but ranked third in the tournament because it requires a supply of verified facts to be commercially viable — it is a long-term B2B play that depends on FactBounty or MeasureGraph succeeding first.

### Ideas Recommended as FactBounty Features Rather Than Standalone Products

- **Real-World Noise & Clearance Facts (idea-067)** — best launched as a FactBounty evidence template; phone sensor inconsistency makes standalone credibility hard
- **Receipt-Verified Purchase Data Exchange (idea-069)** — receipt data is valuable but buyers will not fund this directly; better as a background verification layer

## Category Summary

All 10 ideas share a common thesis: **product information in e-commerce is systematically incomplete, and buyers will pay small amounts for specific, verifiable facts that prevent expensive wrong purchases.** The category is directly addressed to the agentic commerce trust gap — as AI shopping agents become more capable, the value of machine-readable, verified product facts compounds.

**Category strengths:**
- Zero working-capital risk (buyer pays before responder works)
- Compounding data asset (each verified fact reduces marginal cost for future queries)
- Regulatory tailwind (EU Digital Product Passport, DSA transparency requirements)
- Agentic commerce timing (shopping agents need verified facts, not AI-generated summaries)

**Category risks:**
- Buyer prepayment rate is the critical unvalidated assumption for all 10 ideas
- Network cold-start (new marketplace needs both buyers and responders from day one)
- Fraud and evidence manipulation require robust challenge-code and review mechanics
