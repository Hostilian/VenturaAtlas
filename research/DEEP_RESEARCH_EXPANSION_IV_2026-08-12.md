## Six validation experiments

1. Offtake bankability: complete one commodity workflow and interview ten buyers, ten projects, five lenders, and five insurers or traders.
2. Battery underwriting: backtest passport plus independent diagnostics against realized prices and beat age, mileage, and reported state-of-health baselines.
3. Talent Pool last mile: manually process twenty hires across two corridors before automating.
4. EUDR repair: obtain or construct 10,000 messy plots and measure error recall, false positives, repair time, and hours saved.
5. Chip qualification: assess five substitutions with engineers and compare estimated with actual qualification effort.
6. EU Inc prebuild: validate the company-event model with counsel while deferring authority integrations until enacted text stabilizes.

## Operating principle

For each new platform, registry, marketplace, passport, entitlement, certification system, digital rail, or standardized legal object:

1. identify exactly what the public primitive does;
2. identify what it explicitly does not do;
3. map the required state immediately before and after it;
4. measure whether the transition carries economic value;
5. test whether the official rail will absorb the private workflow;
6. seek proprietary outcome evidence rather than policy enthusiasm.
