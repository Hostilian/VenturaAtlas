## Top-five validation queue

1. **CRA Incident Compiler** — replay five historical incidents with 15 manufacturers; measure whether awareness time, reportability, and affected-product scope can actually be reconstructed; require three paid design partners.
2. **AI Provenance Gateway** — run ten real asset pipelines; measure lineage completeness, mark survival, false assurance, and whether buyers want a gateway, a CI harness, or both.
3. **GreenClaim CI** — scan 500 live claims from 15 EU brands; obtain counsel-reviewed precision/recall labels and three paid pilots.
4. **Credential Firewall for EUDI** — test ten relying-party flows against the current Architecture and Reference Framework; distinguish technical optionality from legal necessity.
5. **DPP Bridge** — register representative battery records in the live Registry test environment; map missing supplier fields and obtain three manufacturer/importer pilots.

These are priorities for validation, not a new ranking universe. Their supplied 90–94 scores remain analyst hypotheses and are stored only as `provisional_not_ranking_eligible` research metadata.

## Research method update

Future rounds should test this chain:

`law or policy → mandatory action → triggering event → current human owner → evidence systems → deadline → executable workflow → measurable failure → paid validation`

The method must stop before the last two arrows when there is no operational or commercial evidence. Regulation and funding are forcing-function evidence, not proof of buyer urgency, market size, willingness to pay, or defensibility.
