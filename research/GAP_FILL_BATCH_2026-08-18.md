## Detailed Candidates

### 1. idea-425: EU AI Act Compliance Evidence Gate (91/100, priority)
- **Concept:** Living, timestamped evidence trail proving AI system inventory, Annex III risk classification, and documentation status for SMEs.
- **Why Now:** Enforcement began Aug 2, 2026. AI-literacy obligation has no company size floor. Moving Digital Omnibus timeline requires current-law tracking.
- **MVP:** Intake form + LLM-assisted Annex III classification + generated versioned PDF/portal evidence pack.

### 2. idea-426: EAA Web Accessibility Evidence Audit (87/100, priority)
- **Concept:** Manual-grade WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audit producing a defensible evidence pack, bypassing discredited overlay widgets.
- **Why Now:** European Accessibility Act applied since June 28, 2025; 2026 active national supervision (Carrefour €500/day fine). Automated scanners miss 70%+ of WCAG issues.
- **MVP:** Hybrid scan + human review of non-scannable criteria + scored evidence report.

### 3. idea-427: Care-Home Evidence Freshness Gate (82/100, watch)
- **Concept:** Surfaces inspection rating age prominently and layers recent public signals (complaints, job turnover) for UK residential care homes.
- **Why Now:** CQC inspection backlog creates a 7-year average gap between inspections. Ratings run years out of date.
- **MVP:** CQC rating aggregator highlighting inspection age + public signal overlay.

### 4. idea-428: Cross-Border EU Rental Evidence Gate (86/100, priority)
- **Concept:** Document and listing verification layer for cross-border EU renters (Erasmus students, remote workers, expats) before deposit transfer.
- **Why Now:** €1.75B rental fraud in Netherlands (2024); Italy national ID (CIN) mandate; single-country alert tools fail cross-border movers.
- **MVP:** Verification checklist service for top Erasmus destination corridors (e.g. CZ ↔ DE / NL).

### 5. idea-429: EU Job-Posting Trust Gate (84/100, priority)
- **Concept:** Combined ghost-job verification check and EU Pay Transparency Directive compliance check for EU job postings.
- **Why Now:** 18–27% ghost-job rate; Pay Transparency Directive transposition deadline passed June 7, 2026.
- **MVP:** Evidence-audit-trail ghost-job detector + salary range disclosure checker.

### 6. idea-430: Evidence-Based Fitness Claim Checker (76/100, watch)
- **Concept:** Sports-science evidence verification for workout programs and exercise claims (not credential lookup).
- **Why Now:** 80% of personal training clients do not know required qualifications; widespread exercise myth confusion.

### 7. idea-431: Continental-EU Vet Pricing Transparency Network (73/100, watch)
- **Concept:** Vet bill fairness comparison and price transparency for pet owners outside the UK.
- **Why Now:** UK CMA investigation driving UK vet price transparency (VetsCompared); continental EU remains opaque.
