# Gap Hunt — Round 2

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Run type:** GAP_HUNT / NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE  
**Outcome:** No promotion. All five investigated verticals were set aside.

## Method

This round tested five additional verticals against the repository's adversarial bar: current forcing function or fragmented cross-border data, observable pain, reachable buyer, and no mature incumbent already owning the workflow.

## Decisions

| Vertical | Decision | Evidence-based reason |
|---|---|---|
| Home renovation / contractor scams | REJECT | Sources were US/Canada-specific; Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, and licensing lookups already serve the workflow. No EU wedge was found. |
| Childcare / daycare quality evidence | REJECT | EU literature describes an availability gap, not an evidence gap. Scotland's visit-timing gap did not establish that families lack the information. |
| Investment / crypto scam verification | REJECT | Serious and large problem, but official lookup tools, free tools, and well-funded specialists already address it. |
| Used-car odometer fraud | REJECT_GENERIC / NEEDS_NARROW_EU_RESEARCH | Carlytics already cross-references Czech STK records; CARFAX Europe and carVertical are mature incumbents. A Czech-language explainer layer could be researched without competing on data infrastructure. |
| International moving-company scams | REJECT | Credible evidence was US-federal-system-specific and Relocately already covers the general comparison workflow. |

No candidate from this round is canonical, validated, ranking-eligible, or promotion-ready.

## Negative evidence retained

The round confirms that large, old, universally recognized consumer problems are not automatically open opportunities. Raw loss size is a competitive-intensity signal: investment fraud and odometer fraud looked attractive on pain severity but failed the whitespace test.

## Reusable playbook

1. Track EU regulatory deadlines and deadline changes directly. Omnibus-style deferrals can create a short tooling window after the legal state changes.
2. Test the cross-border fragmentation pattern country by country. A gap requires independent point solutions in multiple countries with no useful cross-border aggregation.
3. Treat round-number loss estimates as a prompt to audit funding and incumbents before doing more ideation.
4. Re-test Czech/CEE language and workflow localization as a layer over existing infrastructure, not as a reason to rebuild a taken data network.
5. Re-run stale-official-inspection-data hypotheses one regulated inspection regime at a time; the care-home result does not generalize automatically to restaurants or childcare.

## Research boundary

This is title-plus-external-research evidence, not validation of the repository's 60 idea write-ups. No payment, interview, customer commitment, or experiment receipt was created. The round should inform future trigger-led research and should not increase the idea count.
