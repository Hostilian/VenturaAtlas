## Executive conclusion

### Winner: AidGraph (parent) / AidHeadroom (first product)

Score: 8.7/10. Status: PAYMENT VALIDATE NOW.

Since 1 January 2026, general de minimis awards must be recorded in a central register. The critical insight is that the allowance applies not to the applying legal entity but potentially to a network of linked entities forming one single undertaking under Regulation 2023/2831.

EU Council feedback confirms the single-undertaking concept creates disproportionate administrative burden. Czechia's 3 August 2026 register modernisation added bulk XLSX/CSV upload of linked enterprises specifically to address group-resolution complexity.
