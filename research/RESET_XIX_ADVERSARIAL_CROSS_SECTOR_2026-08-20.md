## Executive Summary & Core Diagnosis

RESET XIX fundamentally questions the hidden assumptions of previous research sprints:
1. **The Regulatory Discovery Bias:** Earlier resets heavily over-weighted mandatory regulatory interfaces because laws provide crisp compliance deadlines, verifiable citations, and obvious API requirements. While real, regulatory ideas frequently suffer from administrative sunsetting, government-provided free reference tooling, and high compliance friction.
2. **The AI Monoculture Trap:** An AI research loop naturally discovers AI developer tools, agent frameworks, prompt registries, and LLM observability platforms. These are the easiest markets to imagine and document, but they face brutal platform absorption from OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google, GitHub, and Cursor.
3. **The Epistemic Gap in VenturaAtlas:** The repository contains 319 canonical ideas and **0 active experiments**. Treating a 91.2 desk-research score (e.g., FactBounty) as equivalent to a validated commercial asset creates severe epistemic distortion.

### The Shift: Second-Order Risk & Physical Reality Infrastructure
Value is moving one layer downstream from raw capability generation:
- *AI coding is commoditizing* -> Value moves to **Multi-Agent Semantic Contract Coordination** and **Architectural Invariant Preservation**.
- *Robotics foundation models are well-funded* -> Value moves to **Independent Embodiment Compatibility & Acceptance Testing**.
- *AI agents are gaining financial authority* -> Value moves to **Trace-Economic Underwriting Telemetry & Risk Transfer (Agent Risk Ledger)**.

## The 12 Kill Questions (Pre-Score Filter)

Before assigning provisional scores, every thesis was evaluated against twelve binary kill questions:

| # | Question | Core Filter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Why Now?** | Has a fundamental technological, physical, or economic condition changed? |
| 2 | **Exact Buyer?** | Can we name the specific job title and budget holder (not generic "companies")? |
| 3 | **Actual Pain?** | Is there verifiable empirical evidence of operational failure, or merely analyst projection? |
| 4 | **Frequency?** | Does the problem recur on a daily/transactional basis, or is it a one-time event? |
| 5 | **Economic Consequence?** | What measurable monetary loss or delay occurs if this problem remains unsolved? |
| 6 | **Incumbent Workflow?** | Who currently owns the user's primary interface or data pipeline? |
| 7 | **Platform Threat?** | Can Microsoft, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, SAP, or GitHub absorb this as a native feature? |
| 8 | **Open-Source Substitute?** | Can an engineering team solve 80% of the pain with free open-source libraries? |
| 9 | **Human / Consultant Substitute?** | Is an Excel sheet or standard consulting engagement more trusted than new software? |
| 10 | **Distribution Accessibility?** | Can an early-stage team acquire initial customers without multi-year enterprise sales cycles? |
| 11 | **7-Day Falsification Test?** | Can a deterministic experiment falsify customer demand within one week? |
| 12 | **Compounding Asset?** | What proprietary data, graph, or network effect becomes more defensible after 1,000 runs? |
