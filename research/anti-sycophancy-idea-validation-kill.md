# Anti-sycophancy AI idea validation — kill record

Decision date: 21 August 2026  
Decision: **KILL / do not stage as a Venture Atlas candidate**

Evidence status: verdict retained after claim-by-claim recheck on 21 August 2026. Some claims in the supplied brief were corrected or downgraded below.

## Hypothesis rejected

Sell research-grounded, source-cited, adversarial AI business-idea validation to solo founders who dislike flattering model feedback.

## Why the problem is real

Model sycophancy is a genuine product-quality problem. The workflow used by Venture Atlas—independent research, contradiction checks, preserved decisions, competitor review, and explicit kill tests—is useful as an internal operating discipline.

OpenAI officially documented and rolled back the April 2025 GPT-4o update because it had become overly flattering or agreeable. Independent research also supports the broader phenomenon, but the supplied brief's specific “88% agreement when users state a preference” statistic could not be matched to a supporting study and must not be repeated as verified.

## Why the company should not be built

1. The core job is one general-model prompt away from free.
2. ValidatorAI claims 300,000+ **founder interactions**; this is not the same as 300,000 unique founders or paying users.
3. Preuve AI publicly describes ten agents, 60+ live sources, source-linked evidence, competitor mapping, and a $29 report.
4. Brutally.ai directly markets honest/no-sycophancy business-idea evaluation.
5. Franks already sells the blunt “straight verdict, no fluff” angle for €19.
6. Open-source anti-sycophancy protocols and runtime circuit-breaker implementations exist.
7. A public Franks postmortem reported users but no paying customers after pricing.
8. Speed is the dominant category promise, while rigor is expensive and slow.
9. General research products can absorb the workflow.
10. No proprietary asset is created unless the product accumulates a real outcomes dataset, which is not established.

The kill decision therefore rests on verified direct competition (ValidatorAI, Preuve, Brutally.ai, Franks), free general-model substitution, open-source mitigation, and the Franks postmortem. It does not rely on the unverified names or the 88% statistic.

## Falsification already sufficient for this stage

The category has multiple direct competitors, a close open-source substitute, and a negative public willingness-to-pay signal. More feature ideation is not warranted.

## Salvaged internal capability

Keep anti-sycophancy as an Atlas governance rule and evaluation protocol:

- require a strongest case against the idea;
- require named incumbents and platform threats;
- separate Thesis Score, Evidence Confidence, and Market Proof;
- preserve kill evidence;
- never allow AI-generated analysis to increase Market Proof;
- require one experiment capable of reducing the thesis score by at least 15 points.

This is an operating advantage for Venture Atlas, not a new standalone startup candidate.

The supplied brief observed that time and slow research may be the only underused differentiator because competitors emphasize seconds-to-report. That does not rescue the startup: slow research is a mode available from general-purpose research systems, has difficult solo-founder unit economics, and still lacks a proprietary outcome dataset. Preserve rigor internally; do not repackage “slower reports” as a narrowed retry of this candidate.
