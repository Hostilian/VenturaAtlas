### Cross-cutting negative shock

The Commission's current simplification tracker records adopted CBAM changes removing obligations from approximately 182,000 (about 90%) importers and adopted CSRD/CSDDD narrowing or postponement. This is retained as a general regression hazard: an announced compliance population is not durable demand. Every regulation-led reachable-market model must include scope contraction, delay, public tooling, incumbent bundling, and non-enforcement scenarios.

## Strongest disconfirmations retained

- Public infrastructure absorbs EHDS search/request submission and the future generic repair-discovery layer.
- Accredited private platforms own France's regulated invoice transport; a non-accredited tool cannot own the core rail.
- Published e-invoice standards and downloadable interface artefacts commoditize basic schema mapping.
- CATCH contingency templates absorb a simple outage fallback.
- Repair coordination requires several parties whose readiness and ability to compel one another remain unknown.
- Commercial research marketplaces and facility-owned review/safety controls absorb the broad LabGrid thesis.
- OEM consumables selectors provide discovery but leave suitability liability with the laboratory.
- EU procurement primitives absorb tender normalization and submission rails; 3PL audit/billing vendors directly implement both sides of activity-to-invoice checking.

Validation-funnel events remain zero. No source or desk judgment is promoted to an interview, design partner, payment, or repeat-payment state.
