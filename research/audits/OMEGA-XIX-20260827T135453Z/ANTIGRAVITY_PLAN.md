### C. Evidence pass

For each idea, search official product, documentation, standards, or repository pages. Record only the capability the page supports. Never turn a vendor’s marketing claim into market share, legal sufficiency, or customer willingness to pay. For regulation and accessibility, separate a technical standard from jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

### D. CHESSBOARD snapshot pass

Use one stable structure per idea: boundary → actors/substitutes → control points/dependencies → moat candidate + falsifier → anti-moat + falsifier → next action → score audit. This makes omissions visible and allows a later reviewer to compare snapshots without pretending the ideas are equivalent.

### E. Human-only reconciliation

Treat MERCURY’s €5 consented shopper test and CHESSBOARD’s paid narrow-vertical concierge request as one first transaction with two evidence owners. Require the CHESSBOARD fields and stop on refusal; only a positive first transaction unlocks the 20-request cohort.

### F. Track A handoff

Return proposals in the audit only. Track A decides whether to apply ownership rows or queue scores. No canonical value is edited by this run.

## Stop/kill rules

- Stop a snapshot if a competitor cannot be supported by a live URL.
- Stop a legal/compliance claim if the source is a vendor page without an applicable authority or standard.
- Stop a demand claim at `UNKNOWN` until a buyer pays or gives a behaviorally binding commitment.
- Kill the 20-request FactBounty cohort after a refused first payment.
- Reject any attempt to mass-produce this format to 324 ideas without a separate capacity and review gate.
