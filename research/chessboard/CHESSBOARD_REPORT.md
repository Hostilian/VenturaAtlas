## E. Value-chain architecture

The FactBounty snapshot maps eight layers:

1. purchase blocker discovery and question formation;
2. evidence request and checklist specification;
3. exact-product purchaser or local-verifier matching;
4. physical observation and capture;
5. submission review and claim limitation;
6. buyer payment, responder KYC/payout, refund, and dispute handling;
7. variant normalization, storage, and evidence reuse;
8. delivery at a product page, search result, assistant, or external evidence card.

The map separates value creation from value capture. Fresh observation can create buyer value while retailers, shopping platforms, payment rails, or purchaser-routing vendors capture distribution and transaction economics.

## J. Moat mechanism system

No moat is marked established. The strongest candidate is a neutral cross-retailer, variant-resolved ledger for “orphan attributes” absent from manufacturer, merchant, GS1, reviews, and free assistants, coupled to an opt-in exact-owner routing graph. Its status is conditional/scenario, not fact.

Required conditions include lawful reuse, reliable variant identity and freshness, repeated demand for the same evidence, measurable improvement in fulfillment economics or buyer outcome, a buyer or agent who values neutrality, and resistance to public copying. The falsifier is a cohort in which proofs remain one-off or stale, reuse does not improve cost or outcome, or incumbents cover the same attributes for free.

The proposed cross-side network is explicitly `UNKNOWN`: both value arrows, side-specific multi-homing, portability, congestion, exact-variant density, and participant reuse must be measured.

## K. Anti-moat system

Three negative-compounding mechanisms are explicit:

- category expansion multiplies variant identity, instructions, safety, moderation, and dispute complexity;
- one-off responders multiply KYC, payout, support, and fraud overhead without necessarily increasing future liquidity;
- public evidence can be copied and summarized by shopping platforms, while private evidence loses discovery and reuse.

Each anti-moat has a growth trigger, negative effect, scaling behavior, mitigations, evidence, counterevidence, linked claims, state, and falsifier.

## L. Commoditization system

Four capabilities are tracked without a cosmetic radar score: basic product-answer retrieval, capture/provenance, AI checklist review, and generic crowd-task orchestration. The larger market threat is that known specifications, comparison, compatibility, and review synthesis are already free on incumbent shopping surfaces. The residual paid job must therefore require a genuinely new physical observation.

## S. Strongest incumbent response discovered

`SCENARIO`: Amazon extends its already-free shopping assistant and community Q&A into a purchaser-routed “show me” mode. It identifies exact purchasers from order history, requests a checklist-bound photo or video, rewards the responder with loyalty credit, moderates the result once, and displays it on the product page and in Alexa for Shopping.

Ability comes from identity, purchase history, product pages, reviews, Q&A, billing, loyalty, and shopping distribution. Incentive is improved conversion and fewer returns. Constraints are real: fresh commissioned physical capture is not currently documented, operational exceptions remain costly, and credible cross-retailer neutrality conflicts with Amazon’s ecosystem position. The response is therefore plausible, not observed.

## T. Strongest candidate moat

`SCENARIO`, conditional: neutral cross-retailer evidence for fresh variant-specific orphan attributes, plus lawful routing and reuse history. The compounding loop is valid only if later queries reuse the same proof or reach the right owner faster, measurably improving cost or buyer outcome.

Attackers include Amazon, Google, retail UGC vendors, retailer internal teams, and field-task networks. They already possess important assets, so replication cost and time remain `UNKNOWN`. The falsifier is no repeated decision-relevant reuse, no economic improvement, or free incumbent coverage of the same attributes.

## U. Strongest anti-moat

`MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`: category and responder expansion can compound against the venture. More categories add sparse variant combinations, protocols, ambiguity, safety, moderation, and disputes; more one-off responders add KYC, payout, support, and fraud overhead without necessarily improving future matching. This mechanism directly attacks the canonical assumption that generic network size creates value.

The falsifier is observed decline in matching, review, support, payout, and dispute cost per completed proof as real volume and category breadth increase.

## V. Largest commoditization threat

`SOURCE_SUPPORTED_INFERENCE`: the capture/provenance and AI review layer is becoming commodity-like. Free products already provide guided live capture, proof codes, timestamps, and hashes; verification vendors provide randomized video and replay checks; C2PA standardizes content credentials; multimodal APIs make extraction and checklist review broadly available.

FactBounty can use those inputs, but it cannot count them as attacker-resistant. Remaining differentiation must come from access to fresh physical reality, variant semantics, neutrality, or measured reuse—and none is established yet.

## W. Most dangerous dependency

The most dangerous structural dependency is exact-SKU purchaser and product-page access controlled by retailers. It is very high criticality for a broad marketplace and difficult to reproduce from aggregate responder sign-ups. Alternatives are field-task networks, sellers, store staff, permission-respecting communities, merchant partnerships, or a narrow concierge model; their exact hit rate, cost, and switching process are unmeasured.

The most dangerous vendor dependency at the €5 hypothesis is Stripe Connect or an equivalent marketplace payment/KYC rail because fixed costs, account verification, payouts, refunds, disputes, and negative-balance rules can consume or block the transaction. Switching cost and exact alternative are `UNKNOWN` until a payout architecture is tested.

## X. Structural kill scenario

`SCENARIO`: a retailer or UGC vendor launches free verified-purchaser photo/video answers; Amazon, Google, and other shopping agents surface them; free tools and C2PA make capture provenance ordinary. Buyers continue using seller contact, communities, assistants, and return remedies. FactBounty remains off-PDP, pays to acquire both sides, discovers most evidence is one-off, and cannot clear fair responder compensation plus payment and moderation cost at €5.

If that occurs and no narrow positive-contribution segment values independent evidence, the marketplace and data-moat theses are invalid. The structural response is to stop, not “move faster.”

## Y. Strategic unknown

The most decision-relevant unknown is whether a qualified buyer will authorize €5 after every free path is shown side by side. This precedes network, data, scale, brand, and automation questions. Current commercial evidence is C0, so willingness to pay, exact-variant hit rate, responder reservation wage, contribution, reuse, decision impact, and incumbent response time all remain UNKNOWN.
