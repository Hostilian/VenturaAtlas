## Green claims: legal claim audit

- Directive (EU) 2024/825 (EmpCo/ECGT) is adopted. Member States had to transpose it by 27 March 2026 and must apply the measures from 27 September 2026.
- The Directive text contains no general micro-enterprise exemption. The micro-enterprise controversy belongs to the separate proposed Green Claims Directive.
- The supplied four-category summary is incomplete. The amendments also address sustainability labels, future-performance claims, durability and software-update representations, comparison services, and other unfair practices.
- “Generic claims without certification” is imprecise. A generic environmental claim is prohibited when the trader cannot demonstrate recognised excellent environmental performance relevant to that claim; the rule is not simply “obtain any certification.”
- The separate Green Claims Directive, procedure 2023/0085(COD), remains pending/at a standstill. A June 2026 Council budget document says no agreement is expected in 2026. It currently creates no operative pre-verification duty.

## Cross-border personal succession: legal claim audit

- Regulation (EU) 650/2012 generally points jurisdiction and applicable law to the deceased's last habitual residence, while allowing an explicit choice of the law of a nationality.
- Denmark and Ireland do not participate, though Danish and Irish nationals may use a nationality-law choice when a succession is handled in another participating EU country.
- The applicable law can determine beneficiaries, reserved shares, disinheritance, debts, and estate distribution.
- The European e-Justice Portal confirms that Spanish common law reserves two thirds of a parent's estate for children/descendants, while also warning that Spain has multiple regional succession-law systems. The supplied British-expat example is therefore directionally plausible but too categorical without residence, regional-law, will, nationality-choice, and asset facts.
- Tax is outside the Succession Regulation and remains another country-specific layer.

## Why cross-border succession is rejected

This is predominantly a bespoke legal-advice workflow rather than a lean evidence aggregation wedge. Outcome depends on interacting personal facts, national and regional law, asset location, family structure, residence history, tax, and document validity. Error severity and professional-liability exposure are high. Official EU portals already provide country guidance, certificate forms, and a free personalized Your Europe Advice service staffed by independent lawyers.

Do not build a personalized recommendation engine, will generator, or choice-of-law scanner. A neutral professional-referral or document-routing layer would be a materially different hypothesis and requires its own evidence; it is not a salvage of this candidate.
