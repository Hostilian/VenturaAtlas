# Research Methodology

The operating method is evidence-first: define constraints, map problems, search broadly, preserve negative evidence, generate concrete ideas, deduplicate by customer/job/workflow/data/business model, score transparently, red-team leaders, and run cheap falsification tests before building. Facts, user claims, assumptions, calculations, projections, and unknowns must remain distinct.

For regulatory and infrastructure opportunities, prefer this research chain:

1. **Forcing function** — a rule, government system, API transition, cost shock, or mandatory standard.
2. **Exact operational moment** — ship, publish, dispatch, approve, register, release, pay, or import.
3. **Machine-checkable failure** — mismatch, expiry, missing evidence, invalid state, wrong version, or exceeded deadline.
4. **Economic consequence** — blocked shipment, delayed project, recall exposure, dispute, or withheld product.
5. **Automation wedge** — preflight, gate, reconcile, simulate, route, diff, or replay.
6. **Compounding dataset** — failure patterns, compatibility results, supplier reliability, external-state history, or process timing.

A new law alone is not an opportunity. Prefer the operational failure between the rule and what a company must do next; reject generic regulation chatbots, dashboards, and renamed duplicates.
