# Research Methodology

The operating method is evidence-first: define constraints, map problems, search broadly, preserve negative evidence, generate concrete ideas, deduplicate by customer/job/workflow/data/business model, score transparently, red-team leaders, and run cheap falsification tests before building. Facts, user claims, assumptions, calculations, projections, and unknowns must remain distinct.

For regulatory and infrastructure opportunities, prefer this research chain:

1. **Forcing function** — a rule, government system, API transition, cost shock, or mandatory standard.
2. **Exact operational moment** — ship, publish, dispatch, approve, register, release, pay, or import.
3. **Machine-checkable failure** — mismatch, expiry, missing evidence, invalid state, wrong version, or exceeded deadline.
4. **Economic consequence** — blocked shipment, delayed project, recall exposure, dispute, or withheld product.
5. **Automation wedge** — preflight, gate, reconcile, simulate, route, diff, or replay.
6. **Compounding dataset** — failure patterns, compatibility results, supplier reliability, external-state history, or process timing.

A new law alone is not an opportunity. Prefer the operational failure between the rule and what a company must do next; reject generic regulation chatbots, dashboards, and renamed duplicates.

Before scoping a lean product, add two filters:

7. **Adjacent ecosystem test** — a new deadline does not imply whitespace when an older, funded category can repoint existing products at it. Search the adjacent industry and current feature sets before scoring deadline urgency.
8. **Data-versus-advice test** — distinguish repeatable evidence/data movement from conclusions that depend on interacting personal facts and professional judgment. Data problems may support a bounded tool; bespoke high-liability advice problems usually require licensed professionals and should be rejected unless the product clearly stays on the workflow/evidence side of that boundary.

## OMEGA XXI — failure-surface extension (2026-08-21)

For mandatory public infrastructure, evaluate the regulatory transaction rather than the law or endpoint in isolation:

```text
input -> rules -> identity -> evidence -> state -> external system -> result -> receipt
```

Record the versioned experimental fields in `data/omega-xxi-fields.schema.json`. These dimensions are not backfilled across the corpus and therefore must not alter canonical rankings yet.

- **Implementation readiness (0–5):** concept through production validation possible.
- **Spec freeze (0–5):** concept through final, production-testable contract.
- **Failure surface (0–5):** static form through API + certification + state + identity + deadlines + serious failure cost.
- **Failure cost:** consequence of a failed transaction, not general regulatory importance.
- **Government substitution:** how much of the proposed value the mandatory public utility supplies free.
- **Open-source compression:** how readily schemas, clients, validators, or detectors erase the software wedge.
- **Certification boundary / continuous conformance:** whether technical assessment is mandatory and whether ongoing version/interoperability drift persists after certification.
- **State-machine complexity, integration depth, outage risk, evidence retention, and transaction volume:** operational burden that may support recurring reliability value.
- **Technical liability boundary and human approval:** whether the product can report deterministic technical findings without claiming legal compliance.

### Failure-surface scoring rule

An opportunity rises with forced demand, failure cost, machine interface, recurring change, transaction frequency, and integration depth. It falls with government substitution, open-source compression, hyperscaler absorption, and prematurity. Scores must expose each component; no multiplication formula may conceal unknowns or create false precision.

### Public-utility boundary

Never compete with the mandatory public utility. Do not rebuild the official portal, registry, detector, or reporting destination. Test only integration, reliability, conformance, evidence, continuity, and version-maintenance gaps that remain after official and open-source substitutes are mapped.

### Evidence rule

An official sandbox proves access, not production fidelity. A binding obligation proves forced activity, not buyer willingness to pay. An analyst score proves neither. Promotion still requires real fixtures, named buyers, substitute analysis, and paid or otherwise costly external commitment.
