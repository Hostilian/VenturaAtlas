## Outreach prompts

- EUDR: “Are you connecting directly to the EU Information System API? What failed in ACCEPTANCE? Who owns retries, geolocation validation and schema changes?”
- eFTI: “Will you become a certified platform, integrate one, or remain outside? Which requirement consumes the most engineering time?”
- DPP: “Are you building registry connectivity yourself? Would you buy a registry/semantic adapter without buying a DPP UI?”

Paid validation ask: “Would you start a paid design partnership at €500–€2,000/month while we maintain the integration and conformance layer?”
