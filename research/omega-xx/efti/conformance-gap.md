# eFTI conformance gap

## Confirmed from public material

- The platform must sit between existing business information and authority access.
- Certification and conformity-assessment rules matter.
- Implementation timing is hard, but some detailed specifications were still pending at the cut-off.

## Hypotheses requiring interviews

1. Vendors lack a reusable CI test harness. 2. Evidence packs are assembled manually. 3. Version changes create recurring work. 4. Platform vendors would buy an independent validator. 5. Long-tail TMS vendors prefer integration to certification.

## Evidence needed to promote

Three paid design partners, one assessor or platform confirming the evidence boundary, and a fixture corpus showing at least five non-trivial mapping failures.
