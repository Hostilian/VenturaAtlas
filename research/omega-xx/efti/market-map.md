# eFTI market map

The official implementation page states that Member State authorities must accept information shared through certified eFTI platforms from 9 July 2027, while remaining detailed specifications and certification rules were planned by December 2026. The market therefore has three layers: business systems (ERP/TMS/eCMR), certified eFTI platforms/service providers, and conformity assessment/authority access.

## Wedge

FreightConform should be a CI/CD validation and evidence layer for existing software, not another end-user platform. It should test canonical shipment objects, preserve originals, produce operation logs, exercise access/error paths, and export a certification-readiness pack.

## Known public ecosystem signals

eFTI4ALL identifies ABONA, GBK, PIONIRA and VEDIA as prospective platform providers. Edisoft and Ybil market eFTI-oriented integration/platform capabilities. These are prospects, partners or competitors—not proof of a gap.
