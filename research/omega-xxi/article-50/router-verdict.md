# Article 50 neutral router verdict

Current decision: **NO NEW IDEA; retain as a narrow experiment against existing `idea-266` and `idea-386`.**

Article 50 marking duties are real and applicable from 2 August 2026. The Commission-backed voluntary Code covers provider marking/detection and has broad Section 1 participation. This increases implementation activity but also raises incumbent and standards absorption risk.

The provider map intentionally leaves technical fields `UNVERIFIED`. Code-signatory status does not establish a detector, API, watermark, retention period, pricing, or interoperability. Filling those cells from memory would recreate the exact sycophantic error the user asked to eliminate.

## Router survival test

A neutral router survives only if an authorised corpus demonstrates material disagreement or mark loss across multiple providers and real downstream transformations, existing provider tools/C2PA cannot resolve it, and at least one publisher pays for provider-neutral verification or evidence receipts.

## Kill conditions

- official/provider detectors offer interoperable programmatic verification;
- C2PA and open SDKs cover the operational need;
- detection confidence is too weak to support consequential decisions;
- customers only need labels, not verification;
- no customer controls or can observe downstream transformations.
