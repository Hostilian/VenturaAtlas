# DPP backup obligation

## Supported legal core

Article 10(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 requires the economic operator placing the product on the market or putting it into service to make a backup copy of the DPP available through a DPP service provider. Article 11 requires DPP availability for the applicable period, including after insolvency, liquidation, or cessation of activity. Annex III includes the reference of the service provider hosting a backup copy among potential passport data.

## What this does not prove

- that the backup must always be hosted by a provider independent of the primary provider;
- that “escrow” is the prescribed legal form;
- final portability, cryptographic, restore-time, or SLA requirements;
- which party bears liability for stale or inaccessible copies;
- that a standalone PassportVault company has demand.

## Minimum falsification test

Ask 15 DPP providers whether backup is independently hosted, portable, cryptographically verifiable, restorable if the primary disappears, multi-vendor, API-accessible, and covered by an SLA. Downgrade immediately if the market already provides this interoperably and cheaply.

Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj
