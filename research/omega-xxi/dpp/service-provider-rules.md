# DPP service-provider rules

ESPR establishes DPP service providers as part of the operating model and empowers the Commission to set requirements, including possible certification. Those detailed rules are not treated as final here.

## Current engineering boundary

Can build:

- provider-neutral export/import envelopes;
- signed snapshots and integrity verification;
- availability and restore tests;
- source and version receipts;
- access-policy enforcement hooks;
- multi-provider portability fixtures.

Cannot claim yet:

- official DPP service-provider certification;
- universal retention period;
- regulator-approved failover process;
- final SLA or security controls;
- legal equivalence of a restored copy.

Sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj
