# eFTI pre-certification research

The legal/technical surface is real and substantially testable, but this is a **first suite**, not an accredited conformity assessment. Regulation 2025/2243 says certification will assess platform compliance and that detailed uniform assessment rules will be defined later. The Commission still lists remaining specifications and certification rules for adoption by December 2026.
