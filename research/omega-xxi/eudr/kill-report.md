# EUDR reliability rail — kill report

Current decision: **TEST, NOT BUILD-OUT**

## Strongest reasons to kill

1. The Commission already provides UI, LIVE, ACCEPTANCE, API documentation, geolocation testing, and continuing improvements.
2. Generic client generation and validation are open-source commodities.
3. The mandate's claimed formal pre-production conformance boundary is not verified.
4. ERP and supply-chain vendors may bundle submission and maintenance.
5. ACCEPTANCE fidelity and real production failure frequency are unmeasured.
6. No customer fixture, engineering-hours estimate, paid pilot, or transaction corpus exists.

## Survival condition

The thesis survives only if real integration owners expose recurring failures that the official tooling and their incumbent platform do not absorb—especially uncertain commits, reconciliation, version migration, evidence replay, delegation, and operational incident response—and at least one pays to transfer that responsibility.

## Immediate kill threshold

Kill the standalone wedge if ten qualified integration owners consistently report negligible maintenance, official support resolves failures adequately, and none will provide a fixture or budget.
