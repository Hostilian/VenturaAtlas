# Czech-market check

## Correction

The relevant authorised packaging company is **EKO-KOM, a.s.** “ČOV” is not the Czech packaging EPR body identified by official sources.

## Existing English coverage

EKO-KOM already provides:

- an English “How to join” process;
- separate forms for Czech, other-EU, and non-EU companies;
- authorised-representative onboarding;
- English statutory-obligation explanations;
- English contract translations and downloads;
- quarterly reporting templates and methodology;
- public client search and downloadable XML/JSON client lists;
- public fees and support contacts.

This invalidates a broad “Czech English guidance gap” thesis.

## Residual testable gaps

- Can a foreign micro-seller correctly classify whether it is the PPWR producer versus manufacturer/importer/distributor?
- Can it determine when current Czech Packaging Act obligations differ from phased PPWR Article 44 rules?
- Does it understand the narrower current Czech AR wording for specific single-use-plastic distance sales versus PPWR's broader cross-border model?
- Can it transform Shopify/Amazon sales into the quarterly EKO-KOM spreadsheet without misclassification?
- Can it reconcile marketplace evidence with EKO-KOM's informational, non-legally-binding public client list?

Those are workflow questions, not proof of an unserved Czech information product.
