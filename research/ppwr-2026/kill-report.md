# PPWR DIY registration guide — kill report

Decision: **KILL AS NEW WINNER; MERGE EVIDENCE INTO STAGED PACKGATE/PACKPROOF.**

## Why it fails winner replacement

1. The pan-EU same-day delisting claim is stronger than the legal/implementation evidence.
2. The national register transition is phased and future harmonisation may remove part of the field-mapping burden.
3. Several supplied penalty claims are wrong or mix legacy national laws with PPWR.
4. Cheap self-service competition is already active at free, €19, €29, and £49 entry points.
5. EKO-KOM already provides a meaningful English self-service path for Czech compliance.
6. The concept duplicates existing staged PackGate/PackProof work.
7. No seller has paid this project, supplied a failed registration, or demonstrated that current cheap tools cannot solve the job.

## What survives

The Amazon study shows a credible operational fragmentation problem: incompatible fields, eIDs, offline forms, dual registration, local languages, weak APIs, renewal differences, and PRO/registry separation. That may support a managed country-routing or evidence-preflight product, but not another static explainer.

## Re-entry threshold

Run ten sellers through three target countries using official guidance and the four cheapest tools. Re-enter only if:

- at least five encounter a blocking error or materially wrong routing despite those substitutes;
- at least three provide the failed artifact;
- at least two pay for remediation or ongoing monitoring;
- the defect repeats across sellers or reporting cycles;
- the product can remain useful after Article 44 harmonisation.
