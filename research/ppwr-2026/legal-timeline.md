# PPWR legal and infrastructure timeline

| Date / dependency | Event | Commercial implication |
|---|---|---|
| 11 February 2025 | PPWR entered into force | Preparation period begins |
| 12 August 2026 | PPWR generally applies | Real current forcing function, but not every obligation has identical timing |
| Article 44(14) implementing act | Harmonised registration/reporting format | Source contract for future country normalization |
| 18 months after that act enters into force | Member States must establish Article 44 national registers | Pan-EU registry infrastructure is phased rather than a clean 12 August switch |
| 1 June 2030 | Commission FAQ says first reporting under new harmonised rules | Registration pain now and harmonisation transition must not be conflated |

## Marketplace mechanic

Article 45(4) requires covered online platforms to obtain country registration information and producer self-certification before allowing producers to use their services. Article 45(6) requires best efforts to assess reliability and completeness. This is a strong gate, but it is not evidence that every existing seller was automatically delisted EU-wide on 12 August.

Article 45(8) separately tells fulfilment service providers to seek remediation and swiftly suspend service if information remains inaccurate, incomplete, or stale. Product messaging must preserve the platform/FSP distinction.

## Authorised representatives

- Producers covered by Article 3(15)(c)/(d) must appoint an EPR representative in each other Member State where they first make packaging available directly to end users.
- For producers established in third countries, Member States may impose the appointment.
- A 2025 Commission proposal would have suspended part of the EU-producer rule until 2035, but the Council discontinued negotiations in June 2026. It did not become the operative baseline.
