# Validation Plan — HeatProof -- Retrofit Outcome Clearinghouse

## Experiments

- **Most Important Uncertainty:** Will buyers let a third party determine remediation or payment?
- **Riskiest Assumption:** Performance gaps are frequent enough to create recurring demand.
- **Cheapest Test:** One sample outcome statement with a realistic dispute narrative.
- **Fastest Test:** Ask for a pilot on a real install or warranty claim.
- **Interview Plan:** Interview installers, guarantors, housing associations, and retrofit finance providers.

### Interview Questions
- What failed on the last project?
- What evidence did you trust?
- Who got blamed?
- What would you need to see before paying for neutral adjudication?
- Would you let this decide a remediation payment?
- What would make it legally or commercially unusable?

- **Landing Page Test:** Present an exact deliverable and ask for a paid pilot.
- **Smoke Test:** Offer a manual outcome review before software automation.
- **Concierge Mvp:** Hand-review one retrofit and issue a statement.
- **Wizard Of Oz:** Use spreadsheet logic behind a clean report.
- **Prototype Test:** Reconcile three projects against stakeholder judgment.
- **Pricing Test:** Compare per-installation, annual, and portfolio pricing.
- **Demand Threshold:** 2 paid pilots or one repeat portfolio buyer.

### Success Criteria
- buyer trust
- paid remediation review
- repeat use
- labor within budget

### Failure Criteria
- no one wants third-party adjudication
- metering is inaccessible
- buyers insist on internal-only review
- projects are too irregular

### Evidence Before Build
- last-event interviews
- signed pilot
- meter access confirmed
- report accepted by stakeholders
