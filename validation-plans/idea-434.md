# Validation Plan — FlexCovenant -- Industrial Flexibility Performance Contract OS

## Experiments

- **Most Important Uncertainty:** Will industrial buyers contract directly for measured flexibility?
- **Riskiest Assumption:** Sites can reliably deliver the promised reduction.
- **Cheapest Test:** One sample flexibility contract and settlement sheet.
- **Fastest Test:** Ask a plant to pay for a readiness review.
- **Interview Plan:** Interview 10 industrial energy managers and 5 aggregators.

### Interview Questions
- What flexibility do you already sell or buy?
- Where do disputes happen?
- What would make a contract bankable?
- Who signs off on a missed dispatch?
- Would you pay for a third-party dispatch ledger?
- What would make this impossible to adopt?

- **Landing Page Test:** Show one contract and ask for a deposit.
- **Smoke Test:** Concierge review of a real site before software.
- **Wizard Of Oz:** Use manual dispatch tracking behind a simple UI.
- **Prototype Test:** Compare ledger output to buyer expectations.
- **Pricing Test:** Test site fee vs volume fee.
- **Demand Threshold:** 2 paid pilots or one program owner who wants portfolio coverage.

### Success Criteria
- buyer trust
- recurring dispatch events
- paid pilot
- support cost within budget

### Failure Criteria
- contracts are too bespoke
- buyers won't pay for evidence
- program owners already have sufficient tooling
- dispatch volume is too low

### Evidence Before Build
- last-event interviews
- signed pilot
- dispatch history access
