# Validation Plan — WaterSpec -- Quality-Adjusted Reclaimed-Water Settlement

## Experiments

- **Most Important Uncertainty:** Will utilities and industrial buyers care about a quality-adjusted settlement layer?
- **Riskiest Assumption:** Buyers will pay for more than bulk volume accounting.
- **Cheapest Test:** One settlement sheet for a real or representative contract.
- **Fastest Test:** Ask for a pilot on an active reuse project.
- **Interview Plan:** Interview utility teams, industrial buyers, and project financiers.

### Interview Questions
- How do you currently enforce quality and reliability?
- Where do disputes happen?
- Would this sheet be enough to settle a make-good?
- What would make it untrustworthy?
- Who would pay for this?
- What is the current workaround?

- **Landing Page Test:** Show the exact contract artifact and ask for a paid pilot.
- **Smoke Test:** Manual reconciliation before automation.
- **Wizard Of Oz:** Create the sheet by hand behind a simple interface.
- **Prototype Test:** Compare the output to buyer and engineer judgment.
- **Pricing Test:** Project fee versus annual monitoring fee.
- **Demand Threshold:** 1-2 paid pilots or a utility champion.

### Success Criteria
- contract usefulness
- buyer trust
- paid review
- repeat use

### Failure Criteria
- water is bought only as commodity volume
- quality data are inaccessible
- procurement cycles are too slow
- no one wants a third party involved

### Evidence Before Build
- interviews
- sample contract
- delivery data access
