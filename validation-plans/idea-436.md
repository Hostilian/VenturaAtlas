# Validation Plan — AltLine Drill -- Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Escape Route

## Experiments

- **Most Important Uncertainty:** Will pharma teams pay for evidence-grade backup readiness?
- **Riskiest Assumption:** Backup manufacturing gaps are frequent enough to matter.
- **Cheapest Test:** One readiness memo from a real or representative transfer.
- **Fastest Test:** Ask for a paid continuity assessment.
- **Interview Plan:** Interview quality, transfer, and supply-chain risk teams.

### Interview Questions
- What is the last transfer problem you had?
- What blocked the backup route?
- What evidence would convince you the alternate line is qualified?
- Who signs off?
- What would you pay for?
- What would make this unusable?

- **Landing Page Test:** Present one memo and ask for a deposit.
- **Smoke Test:** Offer manual review before automation.
- **Wizard Of Oz:** Track blockers in a spreadsheet and produce the memo.
- **Prototype Test:** Compare memo output to expert judgment.
- **Pricing Test:** Site fee versus portfolio fee.
- **Demand Threshold:** 1-2 paid pilots or a continuous monitoring request.

### Success Criteria
- buyer trust
- identified blockers
- repeat demand
- paid engagement

### Failure Criteria
- customers only want a checklist
- evidence is inaccessible
- sales cycles are too long
- teams will not share enough detail

### Evidence Before Build
- interviews
- sample memo
- commitment to a paid assessment
