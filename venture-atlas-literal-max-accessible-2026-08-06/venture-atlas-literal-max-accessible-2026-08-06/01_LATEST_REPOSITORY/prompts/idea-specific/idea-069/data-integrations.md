# Data and integration research Prompt — Receipt-Verified Purchase Data Exchange

You are working on **Receipt-Verified Purchase Data Exchange**.

## Verified context supplied by the idea record
- Concept: A privacy-first marketplace where consumers monetize their anonymized, receipt-verified purchase data — enabling retailers and brands to acquire accurate, consent-based purchase intelligence.
- Primary customer: Brands and retailers (pay for data); consumers (provide data in exchange for micro-payments)
- Problem: Accurate, consent-based, cross-retailer purchase data is not available to brands through privacy-compliant means.
- Proposed product: A consent-based, privacy-first receipt data exchange with local processing, explicit purpose limitation, and consumer payment for data sharing. Explore DRP (Digital Receipt Protocol) as the technical standard.
- Revenue paths: Take a cut from brand/retailer data purchases; consumer receives micro-payment per dataset shared.
- Main risk: Purchase data is extraordinarily sensitive [s29]; consumer willingness-to-pay is very low (~under $2) [s30]; legal and regulatory complexity is severe; DO NOT BUILD without legal counsel
- Current confidence: 4.5/10
