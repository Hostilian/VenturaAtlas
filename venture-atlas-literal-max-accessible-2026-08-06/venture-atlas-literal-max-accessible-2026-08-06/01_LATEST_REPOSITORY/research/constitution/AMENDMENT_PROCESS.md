# Constitutional Amendment Process

1. Open a constitutional-amendment issue.
2. Quote the exact proposed change and rationale.
3. Identify whether the change clarifies, extends, or supersedes a rule.
4. Obtain the group approval required by `collaboration/GOVERNANCE.md`.
5. Add an amendment file; never edit historical source text in place.
6. Update metadata, changelog, and checksum allow-list.
7. Create a new constitution version and rerun all affected rankings.
8. Preserve every previous version and ranking snapshot.
